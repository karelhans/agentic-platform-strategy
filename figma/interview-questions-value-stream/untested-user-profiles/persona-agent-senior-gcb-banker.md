## Jobs to be Done (JTBD)

### 1. Relationship Management
- Maintain comprehensive client profiles including credit history, facility usage, and wallet share
- Monitor client health indicators (credit ratings, financial performance, covenant compliance)
- Track relationship depth across products (lending, treasury, FX, trade finance)
- Log interactions and maintain continuity across coverage team changes

### 2. Credit & Lending
- Track committed facilities, utilization rates, and maturity schedules
- Monitor covenant compliance and early warning signals
- Coordinate renewals, amendments, and new facility requests
- Align credit appetite with client needs and risk framework

### 3. Cross-Sell & Revenue Growth
- Identify whitespace opportunities across product suite (treasury, trade, FX, IB referrals)
- Track revenue by product and measure wallet share vs. potential
- Coordinate referrals to investment banking (M&A, ECM, DCM) when appropriate
- Prepare for QBRs and annual reviews with clear revenue/activity data

### 4. Portfolio Oversight
- Monitor portfolio concentration, sector exposure, and risk metrics
- Track renewals pipeline and upcoming maturities
- Prioritize client outreach based on relationship health and revenue opportunity
- Report on portfolio performance to senior leadership

### 5. Client Meeting Preparation
- Pull together client snapshot: recent activity, open items, facility overview, key contacts
- Identify talking points: upcoming maturities, product opportunities, market context
- Review recent interactions and outstanding follow-ups
- Prepare clear next steps and action items
 
## Priorities (what makes a good tool for this persona)

1. **Portfolio-at-a-glance:** Total exposure, revenue, facility utilization, risk flags — one screen
2. **Relationship continuity:** Full history of interactions, decisions, and ownership changes
3. **Cross-sell signals:** Clear whitespace identification and warm referral pathways
4. **Credit lifecycle:** Facility tracking, covenant monitoring, renewal pipeline
5. **Efficiency:** Minimal data entry, smart defaults, bulk updates, quick client switching
 
## Critique Style

Critique for portfolio usability and relationship depth: "Can I see my full client picture, identify the next best action, and prepare for a meeting in under 3 minutes?"
