# IB ECM Banker, Managing Director

_Persona type: ECM Banker — Senior (MD), J.P. Morgan_

> "My clients want to see what JPM can do for them...how we differentiate from the rest. Part of that is making sure we have the relationships that let us pick up on potential transactions early."

## Bio

20+ years of experience in the industry. Has established herself as a respected leader with a strong network in EMEA.

Responsible for driving deal conversion, analyzing client market sentiment, and managing transaction flow, she connects with current and future clients in Equity Capital Markets. Excels in client relationship management and strategic advisory, providing senior oversight on ~10 deals. As a key decision-maker across the end-to-end deal cycle, she originates and executes IPOs, follow-ons, convertibles and block trades, and makes the capital-commitment and pricing call on risk trades — working to keep JPM's ECM franchise best-in-class and grow its global wallet share.

## Goals

- Develop and strengthen client relationships to drive business opportunities (a "Vineyard Mindset" — cultivating relationships over time for long-term yield)
- Win mandates and close deals aligned with client priorities
- Expand JPM's global ECM wallet share and be consistently recognized as the #1 bank across capital markets products
- Provide strategic, best-in-class and innovative capital raising advice
- Lead negotiations for favorable terms for clients and JPMorgan
- Operate as "One Capital Markets Team" across product silos for operational efficiency
- Manage expectations and communicate JPM's value proposition
- Celebrate client deal success and pre-position for future opportunities
- Mentor and guide junior bankers

## Needs

- Differentiate JPM from competitors
- Centralized tools and databases for client and deal information
- Timely and well-prepared client deliverables
- Periodic updates from VPs/EDs about ongoing deals and investors
- Seamless communication channels for cross-team collaboration (Coverage, Syndicate, Sales)
- Access to historical transaction data and insightful desk commentary for informed decisions
- Accurate, real-time market news and trends to advise clients with confidence
- Efficient tracking and prioritization of deal progress and client interactions
- A quick, single view of client relationships and portfolio health
- Compliance and risk controls embedded in the workflow without friction
- Guidance on internal tools, products and processes (especially newer platforms)

## Pain Points

- Managing multiple deals and obtaining timely updates on-the-go
- Balancing client relationship management with internal responsibilities and strategic initiatives
- Navigating time constraints while meeting diverse demands
- Ensuring alignment of team efforts with overall strategic goals
- Keeping up with regulatory changes impacting deal execution
- Disorganized notes and lack of centralized storage — duplicated effort and inability to recall prior client context
- Poor or incomplete CRM data — declining use of Dash makes coverage history unreliable
- No single source of truth for topic-level engagement history
- Manual, time-consuming coordination for deal execution and team engagement
- No integrated notes capability — a gap between Outlook and CRM leaves notes in silos
- Overwhelming workload without prioritization tools, risking missed deadlines and follow-ups
- Unclear task ownership and accountability across deal teams
- Inconsistent connectivity across regions, with EMEA experiencing tool gaps
- Investor feedback processes don't scale — manual aggregation of buy-side signals during bookbuilding
- Inadequate access controls, creating compliance risk around information barriers

## Jobs to be Done

### Origination & Relationship Management

- Build and maintain client trust through regular, high-quality communication
- Identify and assess optimal opportunities for clients
- Originate deals and provide strategic advice to corporate clients
- Organize and retain client notes for effective inquiry response
- Track client interactions, relationship health and pipeline timelines
- Collaborate with product teams to share notes and cross-sell insights
- Monitor private, distressed and growing listed company space while understanding the stakeholder landscape
- Identify plausible targets and draw an engagement plan
- Track engagement catalysts (triggers) for engaging relationship holders or client decision-makers
- Capture and structure engagement intel

### Deal Execution

- Offer comprehensive advisory services throughout deal execution
- Prepare and pitch deals customized to client needs
- Identify the best buyer cohort and create investor engagement strategies
- Find the best list of possible investors to bookbuild and execute
- Shape deal structure to secure the desired pricing and allocation outcomes
- Effectively communicate deal details, bookbuilding and pricing strategies
- Coordinate execution across all internal and external parties
- Finalize launch preparation and market communication

### Post-Deal & Compliance

- Analyze investor feedback and create deal momentum for pricing
- Analyze successes and failures to improve future deal strategies
- Satisfy regulatory requirements and protect firm liability and reputation
- Manage file storage and access controls for compliance
- Celebrate success with clients and pre-position for future opportunities
