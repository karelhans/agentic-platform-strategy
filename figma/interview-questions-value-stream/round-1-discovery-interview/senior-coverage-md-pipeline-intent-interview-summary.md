Executive Summary
Pipeline intent is not primarily a reporting, scoring, or task-management job. It is the disciplined stewardship of potential and active opportunities from Idea through Closed. The job preserves ambiguity early, increases challenge and convergence as evidence matures, makes accountability and purposeful next movement explicit, and supports deliberate parking or exit.
The final model is:
Recognize a plausible opportunity or review trigger.
Assemble current evidence and legitimate views without forcing false certainty.
Challenge maturity and trajectory.
Choose an explicit stewardship response.
Confirm accountable ownership and purposeful next movement.
Update the shared picture and future review condition.
The principal evidence limitations are:
No concrete lost, delayed, parked, or recovered opportunity episode was supplied at Q139.
The JTBD is confirmed for the Senior Coverage MD cohort, not universally across product bankers or all GIB LOBs.
The business outcome lacks an approved baseline, target, timeframe, and measurement contract.
Supporting records are evidence-backed interpretations; confirmation of the JTBD does not automatically confirm the value stream, scenarios, actors, or capability mappings.

Confirmed Pipeline JTBD
When managing opportunities from idea through close, help me maintain disciplined stewardship through clear accountability, purposeful movement, regular challenge, and deliberate parking or exit, so the franchise can direct effort and protect its outlook without losing viable opportunities or creating administrative burden, even when client intent is ambiguous and judgments differ.
Required Senior Judgment
Which opportunities merit continued pursuit.
What client outcome should be pursued next.
Whether the opportunity judgment has changed.
Where franchise effort should move.
When to advance, reshape, monitor, park, reactivate, or stop.
Boundary
Pipeline owns opportunity stewardship and the shared portfolio judgment. It does not own:
institution-level relationship quality;
detailed transaction execution;
the preparation and conduct of one client meeting; or
individual task and commitment completion.
Detailed commitments hand off to the actions lens. Client-institution objectives remain in the relationship lens. Meeting preparation and outcome interpretation remain in the meeting lens.
Pipeline Value Stream
Trigger And Completion
Trigger: A plausible idea is recognized, material evidence changes, a review condition is reached, or an opportunity requires challenge before more effort is committed.
Completion: The opportunity has an accepted current stewardship state, accountable owner, purposeful next movement or deliberate park/exit decision, and an explicit future review condition.

Business Outcome And Measures
The working business outcome is to improve opportunity portfolio accuracy while recognizing viable ideas earlier.
Selected Measures
Leading behavior: Earlier idea recognition.
Lagging outcome: Portfolio accuracy.
Supporting signals: Accountable stewardship, deliberate progression or exit, reduced status reconstruction, and fewer viable opportunities lost without review.
Guardrails: Manual maintenance effort, false precision, confidentiality incidents, and viable ideas excluded before suitable challenge.
No approved baseline, target, timeframe, calculation, or causal contract exists. The outcome therefore remains a hypothesis.
Business Scenarios
A. Recurring Pipeline Management Session (SC-GIB-PIPE-01-A)
Trigger: Scheduled review or planning forum.
Variation: Some forums align the current picture; others make pursuit, ownership, intervention, effort, or capacity decisions.
Invariant: Each reviewed opportunity leaves with a credible current judgment and stewardship state.
Primary evidence: Q146.
Open validation: Observe real forums, participants, and decision authority by LOB.
B. Material Change Or Closing Decision Window (SC-GIB-PIPE-01-B)
Trigger: New evidence crosses a consequence or timing threshold.
Variation: Evidence scrutiny, urgency, and senior involvement increase as intervention value decays.
Invariant: Evidence is challenged and an explicit response is accepted.
Primary evidence: Q14, Q31-Q32, Q74, and Q97.
Open validation: Consequence and timing thresholds by product and maturity.
C. Parked Opportunity Reactivation (SC-GIB-PIPE-01-C)
Trigger: A recorded re-entry condition or new evidence occurs.
Variation: Prior rationale and changed evidence become the starting point; ownership must be renewed rather than inherited silently.
Invariant: The opportunity receives a current judgment and explicit stewardship state.
Primary evidence: Q147.
Open validation: Retention period, watch ownership, and reactivation conditions by LOB.
D. Ambiguous Early Idea (SC-GIB-PIPE-01-D)
Trigger: Sparse evidence or veiled client comments suggest a plausible opportunity before explicit transaction intent exists.
Variation: Multiple interpretations may coexist; progress is usually learning or client engagement rather than execution.
Invariant: Accountable stewardship, explicit uncertainty, challenge, and deliberate progression, parking, or exit.
Primary evidence: Q115-Q117 and Q140-Q144.
Open validation: Recognition thresholds across Coverage, M&A, ECM, and DCM.
E. Restricted Cross-GIB Opportunity (SC-GIB-PIPE-01-E)
Trigger: Evidence, outreach, or ownership conflict crosses products or regions while details are restricted.
Variation: Only safe metadata may be visible; authority may involve Coverage and relevant business heads.
Invariant: Accountable owner, permissible review path, and current stewardship state.
Primary evidence: Q142, Q145, and Q151.
Open validation: Safe metadata and escalation paths with legal, risk, controls, data, and regional stakeholders.

When a consequential client interaction creates a fixed window to learn or influence, help me enter with a grounded hypothesis, timely context, aligned JPM posture, and critical uncertainty resolved, so the meeting clarifies intent, strengthens trust, and advances, protects, or deliberately defers a decision, with the changed state carried into owned follow-through.

Umbrella Senior Coverage MD Intent - Confirmed At Q134
When client and franchise conditions are changing, help me judge what is consequential in context and maintain momentum toward the outcomes that matter, so JPM responds coherently while there is still time to influence the result, without confusing activity with progress.
