## Intelligence Triage

### Confirmed JTBD

**Confirmation:** Q91, accepted as accurate as written.

> When new information may affect my clients or franchise, help me isolate consequential change, understand its client-specific implications and evidential strength, and choose the appropriate response and intended outcome, so we act early when it matters, confidently do nothing when it does not, and avoid surprises without consuming senior attention on noise.

**Evidence maturity:** Confirmed.

### Supporting Evidence Themes

- Q05-Q08 established that actionable intelligence can change who the MD engages, when engagement occurs, what is discussed, opportunity judgment, ownership, or a deliberate decision not to act.
- Q31-Q32 established that treatment of uncertain intelligence depends on time, impact, and recoverability rather than confidence alone.
- Q71-Q76 established a sequence of materiality, affected-client context, client usability, action timing, trustworthiness, and proportionate source scrutiny.
- Q84-Q86 identified reinforcing failures from noise, missing client context, and low confidence, and defined the minimum triage required before direct MD attention.
- Q87-Q89 kept materiality, sufficient credibility, response, and intended client outcome as MD judgments, then routed the result to the destination lens.
- Q90 accepted fewer consequential surprises, less noise and manual verification, earlier relevance, better conversations, appropriate non-action, and faster downstream movement as success evidence.

### Catalogue Boundary

The JTBD wording is confirmed. The supporting business outcome, job family, bounded business use case, process steps, and scenarios are catalogue interpretations derived from the evidence above and retain their own `Hypothesis` or `Evidence-backed` maturity. Confirmation does not propagate to those records or to capability support.

## Pipeline Management

### Confirmed JTBD

**Confirmation:** Q117, accepted as written after Q113-Q116 established the intent center, lifecycle, senior judgment, and failure context.

> When managing opportunities from idea through close, help me maintain disciplined stewardship through clear accountability, purposeful movement, regular challenge, and deliberate parking or exit, so the franchise can direct effort and protect its outlook without losing viable opportunities or creating administrative burden, even when client intent is ambiguous and judgments differ.

**Evidence maturity:** Confirmed for the Senior Coverage MD cohort.

### Supporting Evidence Themes

- Q113-Q114 established operating discipline and reduced management overhead as the center, while retaining portfolio truth, franchise effort, outlook, and leakage prevention as related outcomes or benefits.
- Q115 confirmed one Idea-to-Closed continuum and identified idea-to-opportunity as the most uncertain and judgment-intensive phase because evidence is sparse and client intent is often veiled.
- Q116 found multiple reinforcing failures across pipeline credibility, silent momentum loss, late senior attention, ownership, coordination, and trusted evidence rather than one universally accepted root cause.
- Follow-up validation defined discipline as accountable stewardship, purposeful next movement, regular challenge, and deliberate parking or exit.
- Early-stage opportunity judgments may legitimately differ; the shared assessment becomes more canonical as evidence matures.
- Coverage orchestrates the institution-level outcome across products and regions, while relevant business heads resolve ownership conflicts.
- Earlier idea recognition was selected as the leading behavior measure and portfolio accuracy as the lagging outcome.

### Catalogue Boundary

The pipeline JTBD wording is confirmed for Coverage. The business outcome remains `Hypothesis` because no approved baseline, target, or timeframe exists. The job family, business use case, actor contributions, process steps, and scenarios are evidence-backed interpretations and require broader validation, including a concrete lost, parked, or delayed opportunity episode.

## Client Meetings

### Confirmed JTBD

**Confirmation:** Q127, accepted as written after Q123-Q126 established preparation, in-meeting progress, completion, and MD-attention tests.

> When a consequential client interaction creates a fixed window to learn or influence, help me enter with a grounded hypothesis, timely context, aligned JPM posture, and critical uncertainty resolved, so the meeting clarifies intent, strengthens trust, and advances, protects, or deliberately defers a decision, with the changed state carried into owned follow-through.

**Evidence maturity:** Confirmed for the Senior Coverage MD cohort.

### Supporting Evidence Themes

- Preparation establishes why the moment matters, a grounded hypothesis, an aligned JPM posture, and resolution of uncertainty that could materially alter the conversation.
- Consequential progress may clarify client intent, strengthen trust or candor, advance or protect a decision, or validate deliberate non-action.
- The meeting job completes only after the outcome is interpreted, commitments are explicit, affected lenses are updated, and the first next movement begins.
- Senior Coverage MD attention may be warranted by relationship leverage, a forming client decision, relationship risk, a major commitment, cross-JPM orchestration, or protocol.

### Catalogue Boundary

The meeting JTBD wording is confirmed. Its outcome, job family, business use case, steps, actor contribution, and scenarios are now catalogued with independent `Hypothesis` or `Evidence-backed` maturity; they are not implicitly confirmed.
