## Context And Variation

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Context** | A banker identifies a plausible opportunity from sparse evidence and veiled client comments before explicit transaction intent exists. |
| **Trigger** | Intelligence, relationship context, or client interaction suggests a possible need or transaction path. |
| **Starting conditions** | Evidence is incomplete; economics may be qualitative banker judgment; several interpretations may legitimately coexist. |
| **Stakes and urgency** | Premature exclusion can lose a viable idea, while forced certainty creates noise and distorts the portfolio. |
| **What varies** | Evidence threshold is lighter, plural views persist longer, and purposeful movement is usually learning or client engagement rather than execution. |
| **What remains invariant** | The idea has accountable stewardship, explicit uncertainty, regular challenge, and deliberate progression, parking, or exit. |
| **Additional business rules or controls** | Do not require forced probability, speculative economics, every interaction, or long narrative updates. |
| **Exit or transition** | The idea advances in maturity, remains under explicit learning conditions, parks, or exits. |
