# Model revision: every job and every use case, tested

Status: Proposal 1.1, 2026-10-07. Owner: `[validate: name the intent model owner]`. This is a proposal, not a catalogue change. Nothing below enters the catalogue until the owner accepts it and the admission rule in charter section 6 is met. Where a record is marked candidate, the catalogue stays as it is until then.

## 1. What was done

We listed and tested every plausible job to be done and business use case in the evidence, family by family. The families are intelligence, relationships, pipeline, meetings, and the cross-cutting material. The cross-cutting material covers actions and commitments, the umbrella statement, capacity, restricted information, deal preparation and the supporting cohort. Sources: the Round 1 record Q01 to Q151, the confirmed-intent summary, the current catalogue, the eight Round 2 guides, the assessment, and the IBIQ use-case mapping. The five family inventories are in the appendix. They list every candidate, including the rejected ones.

We applied four tests to each candidate.

| Test | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Job test | A job is about the person and the progress they want: "when X, help me Y, so that Z, without W". It stays true if every product disappears. Two statements that seek the same progress are one job. A statement with a trigger and steps is a use case, not a job. |
| Use case test | A use case is about the work. It has a trigger, up to six steps, the judgments that stay human, and one value delivered by a completed run. The same value delivered means one use case with scenarios. A different value means two use cases. Screens, feeds and clicks make it a product use case, which the layer rejects. |
| Relationship test | At least one job explains every use case. At least one use case serves every job. Many-to-many is expected. |
| Evidence test | Cite Q numbers. Confirmed only where the participant accepted the wording. Evidence-backed where Q evidence supports it directly. Hypothesis where it rests on inference, a Round 2 assumption or a product spec. |

## 2. The answer to the question

**A job and a business use case are not the same thing, and the evidence now shows it.** We tested fifty-one candidates. Of the job candidates, 26 collapsed into the five existing statements under the merge rule. They named the same progress in narrower words. Of the use case candidates, four survived with a value delivered that no existing use case produces. The result is six jobs served by nine use cases. The catalogue today has four jobs and four use cases, one each.

The one-to-one shape came from how the catalogue was derived: one statement accepted, one process written to match it. The pipeline family alone now shows the real shape. One confirmed job, "maintain disciplined stewardship", is served by three processes with different triggers: reviewing one opportunity, recognising an idea, and reviewing the portfolio. Keep both record types. Fold nothing.

Two things did not survive as separate records, and the model is simpler for it. The umbrella statement (Q134) becomes a framing sentence rather than a catalogue job. Restricted-information handling becomes a control rule in every use case rather than a scenario anywhere.

- **Firm strategy, people leadership, commercial performance, internal stakeholder management.** Offered at Q51, Q131 and Q137 and never selected. Q137: "more will come but let's move with this." Round 2 question MV04 is the instrument to test them.

#### C. Answers to the six questions

**Actions and commitments.** Both, and the evidence says so directly. Q121 answered "both". Q125 makes the meeting job complete only once follow-through has begun. PIPE-01's value already contains "accountable stewardship and purposeful next movement". The embedded step delivers an owner and a next commitment as part of each parent's value. So it is not a separate use case there. A separate use case exists only where the value differs: resolving a commitment that is drifting or waiting on the MD (Q18, Q19, Q23, Q36, Q120 "commitments resolve explicitly"). That is BUC-GIB-ACT-01. It has an entry threshold (Q17, Q23), not a task inventory. Q141 selected no personal senior-attention plan. Q118 described a combined system with no single object. Together they mean the standing review reads other families' records. It does not own a new one.

**Umbrella (Q134).** In shape it is a JTBD, and the participant accepted it as written. Under the merge rule, "judge what is consequential in context" is the common factor of the four family JTBDs. Q131-Q132 asked for exactly that. So it adds no progress of its own. It is a JTBD only for work no family explains: cross-family situations (BUC-GIB-SIT-01, Q59-Q63, Q141) and daily re-orientation (BUC-GIB-INTEL-02). If we admit those, keep it as JTBD-GIB-UMB-01 with them as its only use cases. If not, the confirmed-intent boundary ("orients the model, does not imply one use case") is right. We should then file it as a framing statement, not a catalogue JTBD.

**Restricted information.** A cross-family control. Its value sentence belongs to the parent. Q70 says controls appear "only when triggered". The rule is the same in every family. Q145 states it: others may know a restricted situation exists and who owns it; further visibility depends on the restriction. Q02 and Q06 show the same failure inside the intelligence family. The layer has no control record type. The home is the "Business rules and controls" field of all four BUCs, plus the constraint rows of the actor records. SC-GIB-PIPE-01-E can stay as the pipeline instance, because pipeline S2-S6 genuinely vary. It should not be the only place the rule lives.

**Deal preparation.** Plausibly a job family, but not a Coverage one. The only banker evidence is Q04 (prep is junior work), Q143 (economics stay qualitative at idea stage), Q140 (leakage runs from idea to recognised opportunity) and Q148 (M&A differs materially from ECM/DCM). All of it describes inputs to pipeline management, not a standing Coverage job. Beyond the IBIQ spec there is no episode. No participant from the cohort that owns it has been interviewed. Record it as JF-GIB-DEALPREP-01 at Hypothesis, with owner "deal team cohort, no actor record". Do not borrow Coverage records for it (charter section 3).

**Supporting cohort.** Yes to an actor record, no to a separate JTBD. Q04, Q22, Q29/Q35 ("first VP amongst juniors, then MD reviews"), Q34 (a standing senior-ready bar), Q86, Q107 and Q144 evidence a VP-led cohort. It prepares, translates and quality-controls in every family, not only pipeline. Its candidate job ("help us move without returning for interpretation") is the delegate's side of JTBD-GIB-ACT-01's own trade-off. So the merge rule applies: write ACT-01 in collective form, with the cohort as co-owner. One record, ACTOR-COV-SUPPORT-TEAM (VP, associate, analyst), Evidence-backed from the MD's account only. Keep product partners out of it. Q148 says Coverage and product bankers differ materially.

**Anything else.** Nothing with evidence. Q137 recorded "more will come but let's move with this". Mobilising the bank sits inside relationship management (Q69). Risk and controls stayed triggered (Q64, Q70). Commercial performance was selected at Q149, then displaced by portfolio accuracy, so it lives as pipeline's lagging outcome. Capacity trade-offs were not chosen as irreducible (Q132). Firm strategy and people leadership were offered (Q51, Q131, Q137) and never selected. Business-head portfolio oversight (Q142, Q148) belongs to a different cohort, which ACTOR-COV-SENIOR-MD excludes. MV04 is the right instrument to test these. None should be pre-admitted.
