# Model revision: every job and every use case, tested

Status: Proposal 1.0, 2026-10-07. Owner: `[validate: name the intent model owner]`. This is a proposal, not a catalogue change. Nothing below is admitted until the owner accepts it and the admission rule in charter section 6 is met. Where a record is marked candidate, the catalogue stays as it is until then.

## 1. What was done

Every plausible job to be done and business use case in the evidence was listed and tested, family by family: intelligence, relationships, pipeline, meetings, and the cross-cutting material (actions and commitments, the umbrella statement, capacity, restricted information, deal preparation, the supporting cohort). Sources: the Round 1 record Q01 to Q151, the confirmed-intent synthesis, the current catalogue, the eight Round 2 guides, the assessment, and the IBIQ use-case mapping. The five family inventories are in the appendix with every candidate, including the ones rejected.

Four tests were applied to each candidate.

| Test | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Job test | A job is about the person: the progress they want, "when X, help me Y, so that Z, without W". It stays true if every product disappears. Two statements seeking the same progress are one job. A statement with a trigger and steps is a use case, not a job. |
| Use case test | A use case is about the work: a trigger, up to six steps, the judgments that stay human, and one value delivered by a completed run. Same value delivered means one use case with scenarios. Different value means two use cases. Screens, feeds and clicks mean a product use case, rejected from the layer. |
| Relationship test | Every use case is explained by at least one job; every job is served by at least one use case. Many-to-many is expected. |
| Evidence test | Cite Q numbers. Confirmed only where the participant accepted the wording. Evidence-backed where Q evidence supports it directly. Hypothesis where it rests on inference, a Round 2 assumption or a product spec. |

## 2. The answer to the question

**A job and a business use case are not the same thing, and the evidence now shows it.** Fifty-one candidates were tested. Of the job candidates, 26 collapsed into the five existing statements under the merge rule, because they named the same progress in narrower words. Of the use case candidates, four survived with a value delivered that no existing use case produces. The result is six jobs served by nine use cases, where the catalogue today has four jobs and four use cases, one each.

The one-to-one shape was an artefact of how the catalogue was derived: one statement accepted, one process written to match it. The pipeline family alone now shows the real shape. One confirmed job, "maintain disciplined stewardship", is served by three differently triggered processes: reviewing one opportunity, recognising an idea, and reviewing the portfolio. Keep both record types. Fold nothing.

Two things did not survive as separate records and simplify the model: the umbrella statement (Q134) becomes a framing sentence rather than a catalogue job, and restricted-information handling becomes a control rule in every use case rather than a scenario anywhere.

### 3.3 Job candidates merged or rejected

Twenty-six candidates merged into the statements above because they named the same progress: not being surprised, confident non-action, recognising ideas early, knowing what is real, remembering parked work, purposeful contact, one-bank coherence, repair, who matters now, preparation only, conversion only, listening, team-level low-friction stewardship, the preparatory contributor's job, market intelligence as a source class, and others. Rejected as jobs, with the reason:

- **Umbrella, Q134.** "Judge what is consequential in context and maintain momentum" is the common factor of the four family jobs; Q131 and Q132 asked for exactly that. It adds no progress of its own. The participant also declined to make it a process (Q52, Q56) and the confirmed-intent boundary says it implies no use case. It stays in the charter and job family records as the framing sentence of the model. Not a catalogue job.
- **Daily re-orientation (IBIQ UC-01).** Start of day is a situation, not a progress. The value a product would deliver, one ranked attention set, was declined at Q52 and Q56. Becomes a scenario of INTEL-01 (below).
- **Worth my presence, placing senior intervention, capacity.** Attention allocation was deferred at Q130 and not chosen as irreducible at Q132. No record.
- **Restricted information.** A control held by the "without" clauses and a rule in every use case, not a job (Q70, Q145).
- **Deal preparation.** Not a Coverage job. The only banker evidence (Q04, Q143, Q140, Q148) describes inputs to pipeline stewardship, and the cohort that would own it has no actor record and has not been interviewed.
- **Franchise strategy, people leadership, commercial performance, internal stakeholder management.** Offered at Q51, Q131, Q137 and never selected. Q137: "more will come but let's move with this." Round 2 question MV04 is the instrument to test them.

## 9. What this does not do

It admits nothing. It promotes no record past its evidence. Every candidate above waits for the admission rule: an evidence record, an episode or a gap marker, the quality gate, an owner, and the business's language. Round 1 still rests on one participant and has no observed episode (Q139), so the three pipeline use cases, the meeting use case and the two new intelligence records all share that gap. Round 2 questions CPIPE03, CPIPE08, CPIPE09, CPIPE12, CINT12, CREL07, CREL08, CREL12, CMEET12 and the whole model-validation guide are the instruments that close it.

#### C. Answers to the six questions

**Actions and commitments.** Both, and the evidence says so directly: Q121 "both", Q125 makes the meeting job complete only when follow-through has begun, and PIPE-01's value already contains "accountable stewardship and purposeful next movement". The embedded step delivers an owner and next commitment as part of each parent's value, so it is not a separate use case there. A separate use case exists only where the value is different: resolving a commitment that is drifting or waiting on the MD (Q18, Q19, Q23, Q36, Q120 "commitments resolve explicitly"). That is BUC-GIB-ACT-01, with an entry threshold (Q17, Q23) rather than a task inventory. Q141 (no personal senior-attention plan) and Q118 (a combined system, no single object) mean the standing review reads other families' records; it does not own a new one.

**Umbrella (Q134).** In shape it is a JTBD and the participant accepted it as written. Under the merge rule, "judge what is consequential in context" is the common factor of the four family JTBDs (Q131-Q132 asked for exactly that), so it adds no progress of its own. It is a JTBD only for work no family explains: cross-family situations (BUC-GIB-SIT-01, Q59-Q63, Q141) and daily re-orientation (BUC-GIB-INTEL-02). If those are admitted, keep it as JTBD-GIB-UMB-01 with them as its only use cases; if not, the confirmed-intent boundary ("orients the model, does not imply one use case") is right and it should be filed as a framing statement, not a catalogue JTBD.

**Restricted information.** A cross-family control. Its value sentence is the parent's; Q70 says controls appear "only when triggered"; the rule (Q145: others may know a restricted situation exists and who owns it, further visibility depends on the restriction) is invariant across families, and Q02/Q06 show the same failure inside intelligence. The layer has no control record type; the home is the "Business rules and controls" field of all four BUCs plus the actor records' constraint rows. SC-GIB-PIPE-01-E can stay as the pipeline instance because pipeline S2-S6 genuinely vary; it should not be the only place the rule lives.

**Deal preparation.** Plausibly a job family, but not a Coverage one. Q04 (prep is junior work), Q143 (economics stay qualitative at idea stage), Q140 (leakage is idea to recognised opportunity) and Q148 (M&A versus ECM/DCM differ materially) are the only banker evidence, and they describe inputs to pipeline stewardship, not a standing Coverage job. Beyond the IBIQ spec there is no episode and no participant from the cohort that owns it. Record as JF-GIB-DEALPREP-01, Hypothesis, owner "deal team cohort, no actor record", and do not borrow Coverage records for it (charter section 3).

**Supporting cohort.** Yes to an actor record, no to a separate JTBD. Q04, Q22, Q29/Q35 ("first VP amongst juniors, then MD reviews"), Q34 (standing senior-ready bar), Q86, Q107 and Q144 evidence a VP-led cohort that prepares, translates and quality-controls in every family, not only pipeline. Its candidate job ("help us move without returning for interpretation") is the delegate's side of JTBD-GIB-ACT-01's own trade-off, so the merge rule applies: write ACT-01 in collective form with the cohort as co-owner. One record, ACTOR-COV-SUPPORT-TEAM (VP, associate, analyst), Evidence-backed from the MD's account only. Product partners should stay out of it: Q148 says Coverage and product bankers vary materially.

**Anything else.** Nothing with evidence. Q137 recorded "more will come but let's move with this". Mobilising the bank went inside relationship management (Q69); risk and controls stayed triggered (Q64, Q70); commercial performance was selected at Q149 and then displaced by portfolio accuracy, so it lives as pipeline's lagging outcome; capacity trade-offs were not chosen as irreducible (Q132). Franchise strategy and people leadership were offered (Q51, Q131, Q137) and never selected. Business-head portfolio oversight (Q142, Q148) is a different cohort, excluded by ACTOR-COV-SENIOR-MD. MV04 is the right instrument to test these; none should be pre-admitted.
