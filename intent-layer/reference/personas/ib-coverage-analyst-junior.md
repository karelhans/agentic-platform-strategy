# IB Coverage Analyst, Junior (A&A Coverage)

_Persona type: Coverage Analyst & Associate — Junior, J.P. Morgan_

> "The draft lands in my queue overnight from the MD's phone. My job is to carry it the rest of the way — iterate, resubmit, and fold in the senior's feedback — with every handoff routed and every commitment approved."

## Key Insight

Junior IB coverage bankers are high-output execution professionals whose daily
work is dominated by material production, data management, and coordination
tasks — many of which are manual, repetitive, and prone to time pressure. Their
primary aspiration is to demonstrate independence and add value to seniors while
building deal expertise across diverse transactions.

_Source: GIB Industry Coverage CRM study (2025), supplemented with publicly known context about the role._

## Bio

1–4 years in the industry on the coverage side — a second-year analyst who first
joined the firm for a summer internship. Excited to build a career in investment
banking and learn as much as possible from diverse deals, with an eye on moving
up or pivoting to an adjacent path later. The role starts with a lot of human
grunt work, but with experience comes sharper, more critical thinking about what
would be most helpful to the client.

Within the product, receives delegated work from the coverage MD and carries it
through to submission. Iterates decks with the agent, resubmits for senior
review, and captures senior feedback back into the deal record — the other end
of the MD's proactive flow: disciplined execution, version control, and clean
handoffs.

## Goals

- Work with minimal supervision across multiple projects at once
- Support large, complicated deals and gain expertise across diverse transactions
- Pick up delegated work cleanly and carry it to a senior-ready state
- Iterate decks efficiently with the agent while preserving provenance
- Keep versions and review states clear (DRAFT → SR REVIEW → approved)
- Fold senior feedback in accurately and route for submission
- Proactively deliver materials and make their seniors' job easier
- Build the judgement to take on more origination-adjacent work over time

## Needs

- A queue where delegated first drafts arrive, clearly labelled with their origin
- A side-by-side deck + agent chat surface to iterate in conversation
- Tracked-changes / versioning so resubmissions are auditable
- Capture of senior feedback (from a meeting/call note) with proposed changes
  attached as a diff
- Compliance routing that is present but non-gating on drafting
- Efficient data entry for financial models, with fewer manual steps
- Easy ways to remediate tech issues that disrupt tight timelines
- Support to prioritise project work and gather feedback through the chain of command

## Pain Points

- Inheriting context for work commissioned by someone else, on another device
- Losing track of versions and what changed between review rounds
- Manual reconciliation of senior feedback into the deck
- Manual financial data entry in Excel — repetitive and error-prone
- Scheduling client and senior-banker meetings drains productive hours
- Frequent tech issues with M365 apps disrupt tight timelines
- Tight turnarounds for client-facing materials, with little margin to iterate
- Difficulty finding similar past materials for reference — duplicated effort
- Finding internal contacts and existing relationships to a client across the firm
- Competing for senior and client time for guidance and feedback on the CIM
- Unclear routing rules for cross-persona handoffs
- Compliance steps that interrupt flow when not embedded

## Jobs to be Done

### Core execution tasks

- Build and maintain financial models
- Draft and disseminate client meeting notes
- Schedule meetings with clients and senior bankers
- Work on marketing materials and pitch projects
- Build and maintain working group lists
- Create client-facing presentations along the deal life cycle
- Prepare newsletters for client distribution lists
- Ensure timely delivery of the CIM (Confidential Information Memorandum)
- Balance one-off requests with long-term tasks
- Find existing client relationships and connections within J.P. Morgan
- Research company, client, and investor information

### Receive & Iterate

- Find the delegated first draft waiting in the actions queue
- Iterate the deck side-by-side with the agent chat, provenance intact

### Review & Submit

- Resubmit for senior review ("v2, changes tracked"); status moves DRAFT → SR REVIEW
- Review senior feedback captured into a call note with proposed changes
- Approve for submission, compliance routed and non-gating

## Tools Used

- **Communication:** Outlook, Microsoft Teams, Zoom
- **Productivity:** PowerPoint, Excel, OneNote, Adobe Reader, Snip
- **Pitching & presentations:** PitchPro
- **Deal management & tracking:** DealWorks, CFIT
- **Research & market data:** Bloomberg, FactSet/Bamsec, AlphaSense, PitchBook, TradingView, Dealogic, Moody's, TradeWeb, and various financial news platforms
- **Networking & contacts:** LinkedIn, Ship

## Journeys

- **Primary:** J4 — The deck handoff (`acquisition-idea`): receives the
  MD-delegated draft (J3), iterates, and carries it to submission. The desktop
  continuation of J3's delegation.

## Public Context (Industry-Standard Supplement)

From publicly available descriptions of the Analyst/Associate role in IB Corporate Coverage:

- **Typical tenure:** Analysts (2–3 years post-undergrad); Associates (post-MBA or promoted Analysts, 3–4 years)
- **Work cadence:** 70–90+ hour weeks during live deals; always-on availability
- **Career motivations:** Build a financial toolkit, develop client relationships, position for VP promotion or exits (PE, hedge funds, corporate development)
- **Industry-standard tasks:** Comparable company analysis (comps), precedent transactions, DCF modeling, pitch book creation, due diligence coordination
- **Soft skill demands:** Attention to detail, managing up, rapid context-switching across multiple deal teams

## Opportunities

Based on the pain points and jobs to be done, key product/design opportunities include:

- **Intelligent material retrieval** — AI-assisted search for past pitch decks, CIMs, and similar deliverables to reduce duplication
- **Automated scheduling & coordination** — Tools that streamline multi-party meeting logistics
- **Relationship mapping** — Enhanced internal contact/relationship discovery to surface existing client connections firm-wide
- **Data entry automation** — Reducing manual CRM and deal data input through smart capture
- **Template & workflow acceleration** — Speeding up client-facing material production under tight deadlines

## Recommended Next Steps

1. **Explore automation opportunities** around the top pain points (scheduling, data entry, material search) for highest ROI
2. **Validate** whether the "finding past materials" gap is a search/discoverability problem or a content management/taxonomy problem
3. **Assess** whether junior bankers are aware of and adopting existing tools (e.g., PitchPro, Ship) to their full potential
