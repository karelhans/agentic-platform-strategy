## Process Boundary

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Trigger** | A plausible idea is recognized, material opportunity evidence changes, a review condition is reached, or an opportunity requires challenge before further effort. |
| **Starting state** | Evidence, client intent, economics, ownership, and maturity may be incomplete or disputed; early interpretations may legitimately differ. |
| **Completion condition** | The reviewed opportunity has an accepted current stewardship state, accountable owner, purposeful next movement or deliberate park or exit decision, and an explicit condition for future review. |
| **Resulting state** | The portfolio reflects the accepted stage-appropriate judgment without hiding uncertainty, and the accountable owner can continue stewardship or route commitments into execution. |
| **Out of scope** | Detailed task management, execution milestones, enduring relationship management, one client-meeting process, and technical system-of-record behavior. |
| **Frequency and criticality** | Continuous and event-driven, with recurring portfolio review; failure can lose viable opportunities, distort the outlook, duplicate effort, or consume senior time. |

## Exceptions And Failure Paths

| Exception or failure | Detection | Decision or escalation | Resulting state |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Evidence is too weak to support active stewardship. | Challenge reveals no credible hypothesis or next learning. | Park or exit; record re-entry conditions where useful. | Deliberate non-active state rather than silent persistence. |
| Ownership is disputed across products or regions. | Conflicting owners, outreach, or assessments appear. | Relevant business heads resolve the conflict; Coverage retains institution orchestration. | One accountable opportunity owner and coherent client posture. |
| Material detail is restricted. | Entitlement or control boundary prevents full sharing. | Expose only that a restricted situation exists and who owns it, subject to the restriction. | Safe coordination without unauthorized disclosure. |
| The accepted next movement does not occur. | Review condition is reached without meaningful progress. | Re-challenge stewardship, ownership, or active status. | Revised direction, escalation, parking, or exit. |
