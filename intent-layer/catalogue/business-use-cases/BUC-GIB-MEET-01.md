## Process Boundary

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Trigger** | A planned or emerging client meeting creates a high-stakes fixed window to learn, influence, protect, repair, commit, or deliberately defer. |
| **Starting state** | Relevant context, starting view, uncertainty, JPM posture, participant roles, and the desired meeting outcome may be incomplete or misaligned. |
| **Completion condition** | The meeting outcome is interpreted and commitments are explicit. Relationship, pipeline, intelligence, and action state is updated as relevant. The first next step has begun. |
| **Resulting state** | The team can rely on an accepted understanding of what changed. Owned follow-through continues without reconstructing the meeting. |
| **Out of scope** | Long-term relationship management, complete downstream execution, broad opportunity review, routine scheduling, hosting, or use of a particular meeting technology. The follow-up client contact itself belongs to `BUC-GIB-REL-02` (candidate). Resolution of a commitment that later drifts or waits on the senior belongs to `BUC-GIB-ACT-01` (candidate). |
| **Frequency and criticality** | Recurring but uneven. Criticality rises with relationship leverage, forming decisions, relationship risk, major commitments, cross-JPM coordination, or protocol. |

## Banker Process

| Step | Task or activity | Responsible role(s) | Evidence considered | Judgment or decision | Collaboration or handoff | Resulting state |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `BUC-GIB-MEET-01-S1` | Establish why this meeting matters now. | Senior Coverage MD, supporting team | What changed, client and relationship context, decision window, commitments, relevant Signals and opportunity state. | Decide whether the meeting is high-stakes and what senior involvement is warranted. | Relevant job family owners contribute material context. | Accepted purpose and urgency. |
| `BUC-GIB-MEET-01-S2` | Form the starting view on the client and the intended outcome. | Senior Coverage MD, meeting team | Signs of the client's priorities, prior meetings, decision dynamics, evidence and uncertainty. | Decide what should be learned, influenced, protected, repaired, advanced, or deliberately deferred. | Challenge unsupported assumptions. | Grounded starting view and intended outcome. |
| `BUC-GIB-MEET-01-S3` | Align JPM posture and resolve critical uncertainty. | Senior Coverage MD, participants, supporting team | Participant roles, message, questions, sensitivities, evidence gaps, controls, and what to avoid. | Decide what must be resolved before the meeting and how JPM should show up. | Assign targeted preparation and participant responsibilities. | Aligned posture and acceptable readiness. |
| `BUC-GIB-MEET-01-S4` | Hold the meeting and adapt to the client. | Senior Coverage MD and meeting participants | Client responses, candor, questions, new evidence, relationship signs, and decision progress. | Test the starting view, adapt posture, and judge in the room what matters. | Participants contribute expertise without fragmenting the client experience. | New client and relationship evidence. |
| `BUC-GIB-MEET-01-S5` | Interpret the outcome and changed state. | Senior Coverage MD, meeting team | What was learned, trust or candor changes, decision progress, uncertainty, and explicit or implied commitments. | Accept what changed and what remains unresolved. Decide whether to act, monitor, or dismiss. | Reconcile interpretations without forcing false certainty. | Accepted meeting outcome. |
| `BUC-GIB-MEET-01-S6` | Make commitments explicit, update job families, and begin follow-through. | Commitment owners, relevant job family owners, Senior Coverage MD as needed | Accepted outcome, commitments, owners, changed relationship or opportunity judgment, and sensitivity. | Decide which owner receives each changed state and what the first next step is. | Send each changed state and commitment to the owner's job family (Q136). Relationship, pipeline or intelligence judgment goes to its owner in `BUC-GIB-REL-01`, `BUC-GIB-PIPE-01` or `BUC-GIB-INTEL-01`. A follow-up client message or call goes to `BUC-GIB-REL-02` (candidate). A commitment already above the drift or senior-dependency threshold goes to `BUC-GIB-ACT-01` (candidate). Below that threshold the commitment stays with its owner. This step serves `JTBD-GIB-ACT-01` (candidate). | Changed state is owned and the first step is under way. |

| [`SC-GIB-MEET-01-B`](../scenarios/SC-GIB-MEET-01-B.md) | Relationship-sensitive listening or repair | Candor, trust, and restraint dominate. | Grounded purpose and accepted changed state. |
| [`SC-GIB-MEET-01-C`](../scenarios/SC-GIB-MEET-01-C.md) | Major commitment or cross-JPM meeting | Participant coordination and commitment handover dominate. | Coherent JPM posture and owned follow-through. |
| [`SC-GIB-MEET-01-D`](../scenarios/SC-GIB-MEET-01-D.md) | Protocol-driven or first senior interaction | Seniority, courtesy and relationship positioning dominate. | [`SC-GIB-MEET-01-E`](../scenarios/SC-GIB-MEET-01-E.md) (candidate, `Hypothesis`) | Short-notice or unplanned interaction | Preparation is compressed into minutes and run in part by the MD alone. Uncertainty is carried into the room rather than resolved before it. | The same completion: outcome interpreted, commitments explicit, first step begun. |

## Exceptions And Failure Paths

| Exception or failure | Detection | Decision or escalation | Resulting state |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Critical uncertainty remains unresolved. | Preparation reveals an evidence or control gap that would materially change how JPM should meet the client. | Resolve it, narrow the purpose, change participants, or defer. | Acceptable readiness or deliberate postponement. |
| Client response invalidates the starting view. | Meeting evidence conflicts with the prepared view. | Adapt in the meeting and record the changed interpretation. | Revised relationship, intelligence, or opportunity state. |
| No action is appropriate. | The meeting validates monitoring or dismissing. | Record the Decision to monitor or dismiss and any return condition. | An owned Decision to monitor or dismiss rather than manufactured follow-up. |
| Commitments are ambiguous after the meeting. | Participants leave with different interpretations. | Reconcile immediately under the accountable senior's judgment. | Explicit commitment or explicit absence of commitment. |
| Notes captured without interpretation. | A record of what was said exists. S5 has not produced an accepted view of what changed, what remains unresolved, or whether to act, monitor or dismiss. | The Senior Coverage MD interprets with the meeting team before anything is sent on. Capture alone does not complete the use case. | Visible failure until S5 completes: the use case is not done and nothing is sent. |

## Outcomes And Evidence

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Success signals** | Client intent becomes clearer; trust or candor improves; a decision advances or is protected; a Decision to monitor or dismiss is validated; commitments resolve; the first step begins. |
| **Failure consequences** | Missed influence window, damaged trust, fragmented JPM posture, unowned commitments, repeated interpretation, or activity without client progress. |
| **Current process and pain** | Meeting preparation, context, outcome capture, and follow-through are spread across people and artifacts. The concrete observed process remains `[validate]`. |
| **Business rules and controls** | Client benefit and right to engage; aligned posture; confidentiality and conduct; uncertainty preserved; no commitment inferred automatically from discussion. Cross-family control rule (Q70, Q145, Q142): when evidence, outreach or an ownership conflict arises outside the group with access, others may know that a restricted situation exists and who owns it. Further visibility depends on the restriction. Coverage coordinates and business heads arbitrate. The rule has no value of its own and is never a scenario of this use case. |
| **Evidence** | [Coverage evidence revision 2.0](../../evidence/coverage-senior-md/confirmed-intent.md), Q123-Q127; sending to the owner Q136; coordination and arbitration Q142; restriction rule Q70 and Q145; supporting team Q04, Q22, Q29, Q34, Q35. The capture-without-interpretation failure is named in the Round 2 meetings guide CMEET11 (draft instrument, not participant evidence). [Model revision of 2026-10-07](../../proposals/model-revision-2026-10-07.md), sections 4, 6 and 8 and the meetings family inventory. |
| **Open questions** | Validate the process against observed meetings (Round 2 CMEET12), supporting-team roles, commitment practices, and variations across LOBs. Confirm with a second banker that the follow-up contact handoff to `BUC-GIB-REL-02` (candidate) and the threshold into `BUC-GIB-ACT-01` (candidate) match practice. Test whether short-notice meetings (scenario E) and crisis or adverse-event meetings vary materially (Round 2 CMEET10). |
