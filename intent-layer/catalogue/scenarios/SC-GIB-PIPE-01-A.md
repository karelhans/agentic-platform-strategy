## Context And Variation

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Context** | A scheduled management or planning session reviews a portfolio of opportunities at different maturity levels. |
| **Trigger** | Review cadence or a planned portfolio-governance forum. |
| **Starting conditions** | Participants may hold inconsistent or stale views; not every opportunity warrants a changed decision. |
| **Stakes and urgency** | Shared reality and challenge create discipline; some sessions also make pursuit, ownership, intervention, or effort decisions. |
| **What varies** | The forum may align status only or make material decisions, depending on purpose and evidence. |
| **What remains invariant** | Each reviewed opportunity should leave with a credible current judgment and stewardship state. |
| **Additional business rules or controls** | Do not force a decision where evidence has not changed; distinguish status alignment from decision-making. |
| **Exit or transition** | The accepted shared picture informs normal stewardship or routes material decisions and commitments to accountable owners. |
