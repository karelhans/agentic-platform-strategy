## Context And Variation

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Context** | A previously parked or lost opportunity encounters the reactivation condition or new evidence that may justify renewed stewardship. |
| **Trigger** | A recorded market, client, relationship, timing, or strategic condition occurs. |
| **Starting conditions** | Prior outcome reason, evidence, re-entry condition, and future-watch owner should be available; detailed work may be stale. |
| **Stakes and urgency** | The team must reuse relevant learning without assuming the old thesis remains valid or restarting from zero. |
| **What varies** | Prior rationale and changed evidence become the starting point; stewardship and ownership must be renewed rather than inherited silently. |
| **What remains invariant** | The opportunity receives a current challenge-tested judgment and explicit stewardship state. |
| **Additional business rules or controls** | Retain minimal closure; do not preserve unnecessary sensitive detail or permanent active clutter. |
| **Exit or transition** | The opportunity returns to active stewardship, remains parked under revised conditions, or exits (`SC-GIB-PIPE-01-F`). |

## Process Variation

| Parent step | Variation | Reason | Actor or authority change | Resulting implication |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| S1-S3 | Begin with prior closure rationale and test the reactivation evidence against it. | Re-entry should reuse learning without inheriting stale judgment. | Future-watch owner routes evidence to the appropriate accountable owner. | Active status is earned through renewed judgment. |

## Evidence And Validation

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Success signals** | Reactivation conditions are noticed; relevant history is reused; active stewardship resumes only when warranted. |
| **Failure risks** | Silent loss of re-entry signal, stale thesis reuse, no watch owner, or clutter from permanently active parked records. |
| **Evidence** | Coverage evidence revision 2.0, Q147 and parked-opportunity findings. |
| **Open questions** | Validate useful retention periods, watch ownership, and reactivation conditions by LOB. |
