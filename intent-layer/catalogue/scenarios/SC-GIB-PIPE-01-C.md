# SC-GIB-PIPE-01-C - Parked Opportunity Reactivation

| Field | Value |

## Context And Variation

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Context** | A previously parked or lost opportunity meets its reactivation condition, or new evidence may justify managing it again. |
| **Trigger** | A recorded market, client, relationship, timing, or strategic condition occurs. |
| **Starting conditions** | The prior outcome reason, evidence, re-entry condition, and future-watch owner should be available. Detailed work may be stale. |
| **Stakes and urgency** | The team must reuse relevant learning. It must not assume the old thesis still holds, nor restart from zero. |
| **What varies** | The prior rationale and the changed evidence are the starting point. Ownership must be renewed rather than inherited silently. |
| **What remains invariant** | The opportunity receives a current challenge-tested judgment and an explicit response with an owner. |
| **Additional business rules or controls** | Retain minimal closure. Do not keep unnecessary sensitive detail or permanent active clutter. |
| **Exit or transition** | The opportunity returns to active management, remains parked under revised conditions, or exits (`SC-GIB-PIPE-01-F`). |

## Process Variation

| Parent step | Variation | Reason | Actor or authority change | Resulting implication |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| S1-S3 | Begin with the prior closure rationale and test the reactivation evidence against it. | Re-entry should reuse learning without inheriting stale judgment. | The future-watch owner sends the evidence to the appropriate accountable owner. | Active status is earned through renewed judgment. |

## Evidence And Validation

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Success signals** | Reactivation conditions are noticed. Relevant history is reused. Active management resumes only when warranted. |
| **Failure risks** | The re-entry Signal is lost silently, a stale thesis is reused, there is no watch owner, or parked records stay permanently active and create clutter. |
| **Evidence** | Coverage evidence revision 2.0, Q147 and parked-opportunity findings. |
| **Open questions** | Validate useful retention periods, watch ownership, and reactivation conditions by LOB. |
