## Context And Variation

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Context** | New client, market, competitive, relationship, or execution evidence materially changes an opportunity. The useful time to respond is narrowing. |
| **Trigger** | Changed evidence crosses a consequence or timing threshold. |
| **Starting conditions** | The prior shared judgment may no longer be reliable. Routine ownership may be insufficient. |
| **Stakes and urgency** | Delay can reduce client relevance, economics, relationship credibility, or the ability to influence the result. |
| **What varies** | Review is event-driven. Evidence scrutiny and senior involvement increase. The next step may need acceleration or redirection. |
| **What remains invariant** | Evidence is challenged, uncertainty remains explicit, and an accountable owner accepts an explicit response. |
| **Additional business rules or controls** | Higher consequence increases source scrutiny. Impact alone does not make uncertain evidence true. |
| **Exit or transition** | The opportunity is accelerated, reshaped, monitored under an explicit condition, parked, or exited. |

## Process Variation

| Parent step | Variation | Reason | Actor or authority change | Resulting implication |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| S2-S5 | Compress evidence assembly, challenge, and response selection around the closing window. | The value of intervening decays with time. | The Senior Coverage MD or business authority may take the judgment directly. | A timely, explicit response replaces routine review timing. |
