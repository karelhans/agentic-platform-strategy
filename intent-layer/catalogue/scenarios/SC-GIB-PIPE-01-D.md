## Context And Variation

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Context** | An idea has just been admitted to shared stewardship through [`BUC-GIB-PIPE-02`](../business-use-cases/BUC-GIB-PIPE-02.md) (candidate) from sparse evidence and veiled client comments, before explicit transaction intent exists, and comes up for its first reviews. |
| **Trigger** | A recognised idea enters review with a learning condition or new evidence while intent is still veiled. The act of recognising the idea is `BUC-GIB-PIPE-02`, not this scenario. |
| **Starting conditions** | Evidence is incomplete; economics may be qualitative banker judgment; several interpretations may legitimately coexist. |
| **Stakes and urgency** | Premature exclusion can lose a viable idea, while forced certainty creates noise and distorts the portfolio. |
| **What varies** | Evidence threshold is lighter, plural views persist longer, and purposeful movement is usually learning or client engagement rather than execution. |
| **What remains invariant** | The idea has accountable stewardship, explicit uncertainty, regular challenge, and deliberate progression, parking, or exit. |
| **Additional business rules or controls** | Do not require forced probability, speculative economics, every interaction, or long narrative updates (Q143). Since the model revision of 2026-10-07 the no-forced-economics rule also applies to `BUC-GIB-PIPE-02` as a business rule: speculative economics stay qualitative at idea stage. |
| **Exit or transition** | The idea advances in maturity, remains under explicit learning conditions, parks, or exits. |
