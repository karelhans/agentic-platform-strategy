## Context And Variation

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Context** | The Interaction may create a material JPM or client commitment. It requires coordinated participation across products, regions, or senior roles. |
| **Trigger** | A material commitment, a broad client agenda, or a protocol requirement makes multi-party JPM participation necessary. |
| **Starting conditions** | Participants may hold different context, objectives, authority, messages, or follow-through expectations. |
| **Stakes and urgency** | Incoherent participation can damage credibility, over-commit JPM, or leave material client expectations unowned. |
| **What varies** | Participant alignment, decision rights, speaking roles, commitment authority, and post-Interaction ownership receive greater emphasis. |
| **What remains invariant** | The outcome is interpreted, commitments are explicit, affected job families update, and the first step begins. |
| **Additional business rules or controls** | Coverage coordinates firm-level coherence. Participants may commit only within their authority. Sensitive context follows access rules. |
| **Exit or transition** | Material commitments and changed states have accepted owners, authority, Views, and a first step begun. |

## Process Variation

| Parent step | Variation | Reason | Actor or authority change | Resulting implication |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| S3-S6 | Make participant roles, authority, commitment language, and handoffs explicit. | Several parts of JPM contribute to one client Interaction. | The Coverage MD coordinates posture; relevant owners keep product, regional, control, or commitment authority. | Follow-through is coherent without centralizing all execution in Coverage. |

## Evidence And Validation

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Success signals** | Coherent client experience; commitments match authority; no duplicate or conflicting follow-up; owners begin the first step promptly. |
| **Failure risks** | Fragmented message, unauthorized commitment, hidden dependency, unclear owner, or post-Interaction reconstruction. |
| **Evidence** | Coverage evidence revision 2.0, Q125-Q126 and cross-JPM coordination findings. |
| **Open questions** | Validate commitment authority, controls, and cross-region variation with product and governance stakeholders. This scenario bundles two of the Q126 senior-attention tests: major commitment and cross-JPM coordination. The Round 2 meetings guide (CMEET10) lists them separately. It stays one scenario until an observed episode shows the two vary differently in preparation, participation or follow-through. At that point it may split. Flagged by the model revision of 2026-10-07. |
