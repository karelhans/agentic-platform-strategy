## Context And Variation

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Context** | The interaction matters because of who is in the room rather than a live decision: a client chief executive, chairman or board member expects senior JPM presence, a new senior client stakeholder is met for the first time, or seniority and courtesy make attendance by the Senior Coverage MD a matter of standing. |
| **Trigger** | A protocol expectation or a first senior-to-senior encounter makes the Senior Coverage MD's presence consequential for access, standing or relationship positioning (Q126 protocol test). |
| **Starting conditions** | The client agenda may be unknown or ceremonial; JPM may hold little direct history with the stakeholder; the right level and mix of JPM participation must be judged; there may be no commercial ask to make. |
| **Stakes and urgency** | Mismatched seniority, an absent purpose or an unprepared senior can cost access and standing that are slow to recover; a well-judged first encounter opens access and sets the terms of the relationship. Timing is set by the client's calendar, not by a decision window. |
| **What varies** | The hypothesis is about the stakeholder's agenda, access and how JPM should be positioned rather than about a decision to influence; preparation concentrates on the person, their history with JPM and the right JPM participants; the intended outcome is often learning and standing rather than movement; interpretation records access, agenda and tone rather than decision change. |
| **What remains invariant** | A grounded purpose before entry, aligned JPM posture, an interpreted outcome, explicit commitments or their explicit absence, affected job families updated, and first movement begun. |
| **Additional business rules or controls** | Presence alone is not an outcome: the purpose must still be stated and the outcome interpreted; no commitment is implied by courtesy; participants match the client's seniority without fragmenting JPM posture; the cross-family control rule applies where the stakeholder's context is restricted. |
| **Exit or transition** | Relationship judgment updates in `BUC-GIB-REL-01`; a follow-up contact hands to `BUC-GIB-REL-02` (candidate); where the encounter surfaces a forming decision or an opportunity, the next interaction runs as scenario A or routes to pipeline stewardship; where no action is warranted, deliberate no-action is recorded. |

## Process Variation

| Parent step | Variation | Reason | Actor or authority change | Resulting implication |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| S1 | "Why now" is answered by protocol or first encounter rather than by changed evidence. | Senior presence is warranted by standing, not by a decision window (Q126). | Senior Coverage MD judges whether protocol genuinely requires personal presence or can be delegated. | Purpose is explicit even when the trigger is ceremonial. |
| S2-S3 | Hypothesis and posture centre on the stakeholder, their agenda and JPM's positioning; the supporting team prepares the person and relationship history rather than a proposition. | There is often no decision to influence and no ask to make. | Participant selection and seniority matching carry more of the judgment. | Readiness is about the person and posture, not a pitch. |
| S5-S6 | Interpretation records access gained, agenda learned and tone; commitments are frequently absent and their absence is made explicit. | A first or protocol meeting rarely ends in a commitment. | Relationship owner receives the changed access and agenda state. | Follow-through is relationship movement, not transaction movement. |

## Evidence And Validation

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Success signals** | Access to the stakeholder opens or is confirmed; the client's agenda becomes clearer; JPM standing is protected or improved; the encounter is interpreted and owned rather than left as attendance. |
| **Failure risks** | Attendance by habit without a purpose; seniority mismatch; a courtesy meeting treated as a pitch; implied commitments; the meeting ends in notes without interpretation; no relationship update. |
| **Evidence** | Coverage evidence revision 2.0, Q126: protocol is one of the six confirmed tests for Senior Coverage MD attention, and the job family lists protocol-driven meetings as a known variation; Q10 and Q14 on where senior presence changes the result. Round 2 meetings guide CMEET10 lists "first senior interaction with a stakeholder" as a candidate variation (draft instrument, not participant evidence). No observed episode. |
| **Open questions** | Whether protocol-driven and first-senior-interaction meetings vary from each other enough to split; how often such meetings end in deliberate no-action; whether the first encounter with a new senior stakeholder belongs partly to relationship management (`SC-GIB-REL-01-A` contextual change). Test through Round 2 CMEET10 and CMEET12. |
