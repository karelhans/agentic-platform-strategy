## Context And Variation

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Context** | The meeting matters because of who is in the room rather than a live decision. A client chief executive, chairman or board member expects senior JPM presence. A new senior client stakeholder is met for the first time. Or seniority and courtesy make attendance by the Senior Coverage MD a matter of standing. |
| **Trigger** | A protocol expectation or a first senior-to-senior encounter makes the Senior Coverage MD's presence material for access, standing or relationship positioning (Q126 protocol test). |
| **Starting conditions** | The client agenda may be unknown or ceremonial. JPM may hold little direct history with the stakeholder. The right level and mix of JPM participation must be judged. There may be no commercial ask to make. |
| **Stakes and urgency** | Mismatched seniority, an absent purpose or an unprepared senior can cost access and standing that are slow to recover. A well-judged first encounter opens access and sets the terms of the relationship. Timing is set by the client's calendar, not by a decision window. |
| **What varies** | The starting view is about the stakeholder's agenda, access and how JPM should be positioned, not about a decision to influence. Preparation concentrates on the person, their history with JPM and the right JPM participants. The intended outcome is often learning and standing rather than a next step. Interpretation records access, agenda and tone rather than decision change. |
| **What remains invariant** | A grounded purpose before entry, aligned JPM posture, an interpreted outcome, explicit commitments or their explicit absence, affected job families updated, and the first step begun. |
| **Additional business rules or controls** | Presence alone is not an outcome: the purpose must still be stated and the outcome interpreted. No commitment is implied by courtesy. Participants match the client's seniority without fragmenting JPM posture. The cross-family control rule applies where the stakeholder's context is restricted. |
| **Exit or transition** | Relationship judgment updates in `BUC-GIB-REL-01`. A follow-up contact hands to `BUC-GIB-REL-02` (candidate). Where the encounter surfaces a forming decision or an opportunity, the next meeting runs as scenario A or is sent to pipeline management. Where no action is warranted, a Decision to monitor or dismiss is recorded. |

## Process Variation

| Parent step | Variation | Reason | Actor or authority change | Resulting implication |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| S1 | "Why now" is answered by protocol or a first encounter rather than by changed evidence. | Senior presence is warranted by standing, not by a decision window (Q126). | The Senior Coverage MD judges whether protocol genuinely requires personal presence or can be delegated. | Purpose is explicit even when the trigger is ceremonial. |
| S2-S3 | The starting view and posture centre on the stakeholder, their agenda and JPM's positioning. The supporting team prepares the person and the relationship history rather than a proposition. | There is often no decision to influence and no ask to make. | Participant selection and seniority matching carry more of the judgment. | Readiness is about the person and posture, not a pitch. |
| S5-S6 | Interpretation records access gained, agenda learned and tone. Commitments are frequently absent and their absence is made explicit. | A first or protocol meeting rarely ends in a commitment. | The relationship owner receives the changed access and agenda state. | Follow-through is relationship progress, not transaction progress. |

## Evidence And Validation

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Success signals** | Access to the stakeholder opens or is confirmed. The client's agenda becomes clearer. JPM standing is protected or improved. The encounter is interpreted and owned rather than left as attendance. |
| **Failure risks** | Attendance by habit without a purpose; seniority mismatch; a courtesy meeting treated as a pitch; implied commitments; the meeting ends in notes without interpretation; no relationship update. |
| **Evidence** | Coverage evidence revision 2.0, Q126: protocol is one of the six confirmed tests for Senior Coverage MD attention, and the job family lists protocol-driven meetings as a known variation. Q10 and Q14 on where senior presence changes the result. Round 2 meetings guide CMEET10 lists "first senior interaction with a stakeholder" as a candidate variation (draft instrument, not participant evidence). No observed episode. |
| **Open questions** | Whether protocol-driven and first-senior-meeting variations differ enough to split. How often such meetings end in a Decision to monitor or dismiss. Whether the first encounter with a new senior stakeholder belongs partly to relationship management (`SC-GIB-REL-01-A` contextual change). Test through Round 2 CMEET10 and CMEET12. |
