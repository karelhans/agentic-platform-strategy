## Context And Variation

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Context** | A priority client institution receives recurring or event-triggered relationship review. |
| **Trigger** | Review cadence, changed institution context, changed markers, or need for account direction. |
| **Starting conditions** | Client agenda and relationship evidence are distributed, evolving, and incomplete. |
| **Stakes and urgency** | The review must reveal quality and trajectory without reducing the relationship to contact volume or a false single score. |
| **What varies** | Evidence breadth, stakeholder depth, planning horizon, and required senior attention. |
| **What remains invariant** | Institution-level judgment, objective, disposition, and owned movement are accepted. |
| **Additional business rules or controls** | Foreground institution change, trajectory, and why now; keep detailed stakeholder and cross-JPM context available on demand. |
| **Exit or transition** | The relationship continues under personal engagement, delegated maintenance, coordination, or deliberate wait. |
