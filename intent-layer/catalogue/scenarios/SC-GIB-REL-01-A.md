## Context And Variation

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Context** | Something material changes inside the client institution: its priorities, its leadership or decision-makers, its ownership or control, or its strategic direction. The prior relationship judgment may no longer describe who matters and where JPM stands. |
| **Trigger** | Client context changed (Q97): a known or inferred change in priorities, leadership, ownership or strategy at the institution. It arrives through a Signal, an Interaction, or market events. Engagement frequency and dry spells are not this scenario; they are `SC-GIB-REL-01-C`. |
| **Starting conditions** | The change is known at least in outline. Its consequence for trust, access, reciprocity and engagement, and for which stakeholders now matter, is not. The client's new priorities can be inferred from signs but not documented with precision (Q110). |
| **Stakes and urgency** | A change in who decides, or in what the institution is trying to do, can open or close JPM's access and relevance. Reading it late leaves JPM positioned for the institution that was, not the one that is. |
| **What varies** | S1 and S2 re-read the signs and the stakeholders against the change, not over a planning horizon. S4 may change the stakeholder. The useful response may be to listen rather than to propose (Q100). |
| **What remains invariant** | Institution-level judgment, objective, Decision, and owned next step are accepted; the Coverage MD coordinates. |
| **Additional business rules or controls** | Foreground what changed and why it matters now. Do not restate the client's priorities with more certainty than the signs support. Keep detailed stakeholder and cross-JPM context available on demand (Q108). |
| **Exit or transition** | The relationship continues under a revised judgment: personal Engagement, delegated maintenance, coordination, repair, or a deliberate wait. A Decision to engage hands to `BUC-GIB-REL-02` (candidate). |

## Process Variation

| Parent step | Variation | Reason | Actor or authority change | Resulting implication |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| S1-S2 | Establish the change and re-read trust, access, reciprocity and engagement against it, rather than reviewing the whole relationship over a horizon. | The change, not the calendar, is the reason for attention. | The Coverage MD keeps institution-level coordination; the support team prepares the case. | The prior judgment is confirmed, qualified or replaced. |
| S4 | Re-choose the stakeholder where leadership or ownership has moved. | The people who matter may have changed (Q95, Q103, Q108). | None. | The objective is aimed at the institution as it now is. |

## Evidence And Validation

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Success signals** | Changed stakeholders and priorities recognised before a competitor acts on them; a purposeful objective tied to the change; no unnecessary Engagement. |
| **Failure risks** | False certainty about the new priorities, reading the change through the old stakeholder map, or treating any change as a reason to engage. |
| **Evidence** | Coverage evidence revision 2.0, Q95, Q97, Q101-Q108, Q110. |
| **Open questions** | Validate with an observed episode where leadership or ownership changed; confirm what counts as material change by tier. |
