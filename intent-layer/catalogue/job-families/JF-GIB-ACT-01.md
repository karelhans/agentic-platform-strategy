## Both, Not Either

The participant answered "both" at Q121. Each job family owns its own actions, and a standing senior view shows cross-family commitments and MD blockers. The catalogue expresses that in two places.

- **Embedded commitment steps** inside every other use case deliver an owner and a next commitment as part of the parent's value: `BUC-GIB-INTEL-01-S6`, `BUC-GIB-REL-01-S6`, `BUC-GIB-PIPE-01-S5`, `BUC-GIB-MEET-01-S6`, and the candidate steps `BUC-GIB-REL-02-S6` and `BUC-GIB-PIPE-03-S5`. They serve `JTBD-GIB-ACT-01` but are not use cases of this family.
- **This thin standing review**, `BUC-GIB-ACT-01`, exists only where the value differs. Here the owner explicitly resolves a commitment that is drifting or waiting on the senior before the window closes. It is a thin use case with an entry threshold, not a parallel family of equal weight.
