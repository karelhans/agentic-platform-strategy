## Wording Note

The confirmed-intent synthesis records the statement in the individual form ("help me ... returning to me"). The proposal rewrites it in the collective form ("help us ... returning to the senior") because the delegate's side of the same progress is evidenced at Q22, Q34, Q35 and Q120 and the merge rule applies. The collective wording has not been put to a participant; it is part of what the Round 2 actions guide must validate.
