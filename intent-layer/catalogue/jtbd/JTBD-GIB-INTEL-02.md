# JTBD-GIB-INTEL-02 - Bring Held Information To Its Accountable Owner

## Record

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **ID** | `JTBD-GIB-INTEL-02` |
| **Parent business use case(s)** | [`BUC-GIB-INTEL-02`](../business-use-cases/BUC-GIB-INTEL-02.md) (candidate); [`BUC-GIB-INTEL-01`](../business-use-cases/BUC-GIB-INTEL-01.md) |
| **Job owner** | The banker holding the information: any Coverage, product, or regional banker who does not own the affected client or outcome. Collective job; the owner closes the loop. |
| **Responsible actor archetypes** | [`ACTOR-COV-SENIOR-MD`](../actors/ACTOR-COV-SENIOR-MD.md); [`ACTOR-COV-SUPPORT-TEAM`](../actors/ACTOR-COV-SUPPORT-TEAM.md) (candidate). |
| **Job statement** | When I hold information that may matter to a client or outcome I do not own, help us recognise that and get it to the accountable owner in permitted form, so the signal is triaged while there is time, without breaching sensitivity or guessing at needs nobody has declared. |
| **Situation and triggers** | A banker learns something in the course of their own work that could affect a client, relationship, opportunity, meeting, or commitment owned elsewhere in the bank. |
| **Desired progress** | Move from information held in one place, with no declared demand for it, to the owner having the Signal, its relevance, and permitted context in time to judge it. |
| **Required judgment** | Whether the information plausibly matters to someone else; who the owner is; what sensitivity permits to be shared and in what form. |
| **Desired outcome** | The Signal reaches the owner while the response window is open, and is then triaged under `JTBD-GIB-INTEL-01`; restricted substance stays protected. |
| **Unwanted trade-offs** | Breaching confidentiality, information barriers, or client discretion; requiring owners to declare their needs in advance (Q07: the participant chose inference over explicit demand); indiscriminate forwarding that becomes noise; or silence because the way to share, or the owner, is unknown. |
| **Current process, workaround, and pain** | Sharing depends on personal networks. It also depends on the holder knowing six things: the owner's priorities, open questions, the Signal's relevance, who the owner is, a permitted way to share, and the expected follow-through. At Q06 all six were absent in the participant's own costly miss (Q02) `[validate with the holder cohort]`. |
| **Related scenarios** | None yet. Restricted substance is handled by the cross-family control rule in `BUC-GIB-INTEL-02`, not by a scenario. |
| **Success signals** | Fewer Signals that existed in the bank but never reached the owner; shorter elapsed time from a Signal being held to its being triaged; no increase in unauthorised disclosure; no requirement on owners to publish demand. |
| **Evidence** | [Coverage evidence revision 2.0](../../evidence/coverage-senior-md/confirmed-intent.md) and the Round 1 record: Q02, Q06, Q07, Q142, Q145. Raised by the [model revision of 2026-10-07](../../proposals/model-revision-2026-10-07.md). |
| **Evidence maturity** | `Evidence-backed for the need; wording not yet put to a participant`. The need is evidenced from the MD's side only; no holder has been interviewed. |
