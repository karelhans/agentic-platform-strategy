## Persona Expression

1. **Responsible for:** preparing, translating and quality-controlling work in every job family. The Senior Coverage MD receives a senior-ready case, and the team can move on a delegated commitment without returning for interpretation.
2. **Needs to judge:** what the senior's starting view means in executable terms, whether the work meets the standing senior-ready standard, and when a checkpoint or escalation rule has fired.
3. **Constrained by:** delegation that may arrive without an explicit outcome or checkpoint, access rules and information barriers, and the judgments that stay with the MD.

> The Coverage supporting cohort is responsible for preparation, translation and quality control on the senior's behalf. They need the delegated outcome, starting view, inputs and checkpoint rule, and the evidence of each job family, to make the work senior-ready. They balance time pressure, information-sharing limits and the judgments that remain with the MD.

## Why This Record

All five IBIQ product specifications exposed the same gap. The work they describe is largely performed by people below the MD, and the catalogue had no actor record for them other than the pipeline-specific opportunity team. This record closes that gap once admitted. The proposal rejected a separate supporting-cohort job. Its candidate statement ("help us move without returning for interpretation") is the delegate's side of `JTBD-GIB-ACT-01`. The cohort is therefore recorded as co-owner of that job and as an actor, not as the owner of a job of its own.
