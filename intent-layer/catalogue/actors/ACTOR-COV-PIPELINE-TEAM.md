## Cohort Definition

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Cohort definition** | Coverage and product bankers who maintain opportunity evidence, steward purposeful next movement, and surface changed trajectory, disagreement, or blockage. |
| **Inclusion criteria** | Bankers with accepted responsibility for evidence, client progress, or stewardship of one or more potential or active opportunities. |

## Work-Relevant Characteristics

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Goals and pressures** | Keep viable opportunities moving without over-populating records or repeatedly reconstructing senior intent. |
| **Evidence and information needs** | Current hypothesis, strategic value, client evidence, accountable owner, next outcome, provenance, confidence, freshness, and visibility constraints. |
| **Expertise and mental models** | Translates senior direction into stage-appropriate evidence, stewardship, and next movement; distinguishes activity from client progress. |
| **Working patterns** | Collaborates across Coverage, product, region, and seniority through recurring reviews and event-driven changes. |
| **Pain points and failure exposure** | Nominal ownership, stale evidence, unclear quality bar, duplicated outreach, missing handoffs, and late escalation. |
| **Control and sensitivity constraints** | Entitlements, client confidentiality, information barriers, regional restrictions, and need-to-know boundaries. |
| **Meaningful internal variations** | VP translation and quality control, junior evidence maintenance, product expertise, and regional contribution `[validate]`. |

## Persona Expression

1. **Responsible for:** coherent opportunity evidence, accepted stewardship, and purposeful next movement.
2. **Needs to judge:** whether evidence changes trajectory, maturity, ownership, or the need for senior judgment.
3. **Constrained by:** ambiguous client intent, cross-team dependencies, manual reconstruction, and information-sharing limits.
