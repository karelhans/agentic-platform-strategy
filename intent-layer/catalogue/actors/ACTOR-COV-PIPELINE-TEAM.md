# ACTOR-COV-PIPELINE-TEAM - Coverage Opportunity Team

## Cohort Definition

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Cohort definition** | Coverage and product bankers who maintain opportunity evidence, own the purposeful next step, and surface changed direction, disagreement, or blockage. |
| **Inclusion criteria** | Bankers with accepted responsibility for evidence, client progress, or management of one or more potential or active opportunities. |
| **Exclusion criteria** | Contributors supplying isolated evidence or advice without ongoing opportunity responsibility. |
| **Business context** | Idea-to-Closed opportunity work involving Coverage, product, regional, VP, associate, and specialist contributions. |
| **Evidence base** | [Coverage Senior MD confirmed intent evidence revision 2.0](../../evidence/coverage-senior-md/confirmed-intent.md), especially Q114-Q116 and Q118-Q122. |
| **Evidence maturity** | `Evidence-backed` - team contribution and ownership themes recur, but cohort boundaries require broader validation. |

## Work-Relevant Characteristics

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Goals and pressures** | Keep viable opportunities moving without over-populating records or repeatedly reconstructing senior intent. |
| **Evidence and information needs** | Current starting view, strategic value, client evidence, owner, next outcome, source trail, confidence, freshness, and visibility constraints. |
| **Expertise and mental models** | Translates senior direction into stage-appropriate evidence, management, and next steps; distinguishes activity from client progress. |
| **Working patterns** | Collaborates across Coverage, product, region, and seniority through recurring reviews and event-driven changes. |
| **Pain points and failure exposure** | Nominal ownership, stale evidence, unclear quality bar, duplicated outreach, missing handoffs, and late escalation. |
| **Control and sensitivity constraints** | Access rules, client confidentiality, information barriers, regional restrictions, and need-to-know boundaries. |
| **Meaningful internal variations** | VP translation and quality control, now evidenced at Q29 and Q35: the VP works first among the juniors, then the MD reviews. Junior evidence maintenance, product expertise, and regional contribution `[validate]`. |

## Persona Expression

1. **Responsible for:** coherent opportunity evidence, accepted ownership, and purposeful next steps.
2. **Needs to judge:** whether evidence changes direction, stage, ownership, or the need for senior judgment.
3. **Constrained by:** ambiguous client intent, cross-team dependencies, manual reconstruction, and information-sharing limits.
