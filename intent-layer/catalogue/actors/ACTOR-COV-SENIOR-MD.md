# ACTOR-COV-SENIOR-MD - Senior Coverage MD

## Cohort Definition

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Cohort definition** | Senior Coverage bankers accountable for institution-level relationship quality, material intelligence judgment, and portfolio management across priority client and opportunity work. |
| **Inclusion criteria** | Senior Coverage MD or equivalent role, accountable for coordinating the client institution, senior client contact, and portfolio judgment. |
| **Exclusion criteria** | Product bankers without institution-level Coverage accountability; business heads whose primary job is firm-wide governance rather than management of owned clients `[validate]`. |
| **Business context** | Priority client institutions, ambiguous origination, cross-product and cross-region coordination, high-stakes client meetings, and time-sensitive opportunity judgment. |
| **Evidence base** | [Coverage Senior MD confirmed intent evidence revision 2.0](../../evidence/coverage-senior-md/confirmed-intent.md), supported by the Coverage role and JTBD framework. |
| **Evidence maturity** | `Evidence-backed` - the role is directly evidenced; this complete catalogue expression is prepared from that evidence rather than confirmed. |

## Work-Relevant Characteristics

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Goals and pressures** | Maintain client and firm momentum, protect relationship quality, recognize viable ideas early, and direct scarce effort without spending senior time on reconstruction or noise. |
| **Evidence and information needs** | Client and opportunity context, source trail, confidence, strategic value, owner, next client outcome, material changes, and sensitivity constraints. |
| **Expertise and mental models** | Judges consequence in institution, relationship, competitive, market, and firm context; distinguishes ambiguous early evidence from mature opportunity facts. |
| **Working patterns** | Operates between meetings, while traveling, in live internal discussions, during client conversations, and at the desktop for complex or sensitive work. |
| **Pain points and failure exposure** | Fragmented context, late idea recognition, stale or falsely precise pipeline views, nominal ownership, duplicated outreach, and hidden dependencies. |
| **Control and sensitivity constraints** | Client confidentiality, information barriers, clean-team restrictions, regional rules, and relationship sensitivity. |
| **Meaningful internal variations** | Priority versus developing clients, domestic versus multi-region work, sector context, and available team support `[validate]`. |

## Persona Expression

1. **Responsible for:** institution-level coordination and senior judgment where consequence, ambiguity, and relationship leverage are material.
2. **Needs to judge:** what matters now, which opportunities merit the firm's effort, and what client or firm outcome should move next.
3. **Constrained by:** incomplete evidence, veiled client intent, confidentiality, limited attention, and cross-JPM dependencies.
