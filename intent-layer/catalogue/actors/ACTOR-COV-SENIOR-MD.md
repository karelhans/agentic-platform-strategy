## Work-Relevant Characteristics

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Goals and pressures** | Maintain client and franchise momentum, protect relationship quality, recognize viable ideas early, and direct scarce effort without consuming senior time on reconstruction or noise. |
| **Evidence and information needs** | Client and opportunity context, provenance, confidence, strategic value, accountable owner, next client outcome, material changes, and sensitivity constraints. |
| **Expertise and mental models** | Judges consequence in institution, relationship, competitive, market, and franchise context; distinguishes ambiguous early evidence from mature opportunity facts. |
| **Working patterns** | Operates between meetings, while traveling, in live internal discussions, during client conversations, and at the desktop for complex or sensitive work. |
| **Pain points and failure exposure** | Fragmented context, late idea recognition, stale or falsely precise pipeline views, nominal ownership, duplicated outreach, and hidden dependencies. |
| **Control and sensitivity constraints** | Client confidentiality, information barriers, clean-team restrictions, regional rules, and relationship sensitivity. |
| **Meaningful internal variations** | Priority versus developing clients, domestic versus multi-region work, sector context, and available team support `[validate]`. |

## Persona Expression

1. **Responsible for:** institution-level orchestration and senior judgment where consequence, ambiguity, and relationship leverage are material.
2. **Needs to judge:** what matters now, which opportunities merit stewardship, and what client or franchise outcome should move next.
3. **Constrained by:** incomplete evidence, veiled client intent, confidentiality, limited attention, and cross-JPM dependencies.
