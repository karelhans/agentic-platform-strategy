# Senior Coverage MD Intent Interview - Full Appendix

## Purpose

This appendix preserves the complete Q01-Q151 Senior Coverage MD interview instrument in question order. It includes the original question wording, response mode, possible answers, probes, recorded response, and status or section output wherever those fields exist in the source record.

This is a historical interview appendix, not a new canonical intent record. Possible answers are the choices offered during discovery; they are not automatically current conclusions. Skipped, cancelled, reopened, superseded, unanswered, provisional, and confirmed questions remain visibly labeled so the research history is not flattened.

## Response Conventions

- `Free text` indicates an open narrative response.
- Choice lists preserve the options originally offered; response-mode instructions state whether ranking, single selection, or multi-selection was requested.
- `Recorded answer` preserves the historical response, including blanks and explicit non-answers.
- `Status` records whether a question remained current, was reopened, superseded, abandoned, cancelled, or confirmed.
- Later questions include audience, probes, and section outputs where the original guide supplied them.
