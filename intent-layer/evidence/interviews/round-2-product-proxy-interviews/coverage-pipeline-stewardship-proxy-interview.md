| Field | Value |

## Interviewer Guidance

- Share all twelve questions in advance.
- Ask respondents to label each answer as direct experience, observed practice, or informed hypothesis.
- Keep pipeline centered on opportunity stewardship, not detailed task execution or one system's stage model.
- Preserve legitimate early ambiguity and avoid forcing probability, economics, or one canonical view before evidence supports it.
- Distinguish Coverage, product, region, client tier, and maturity variations.
- Proxy responses establish candidate direction only. They do not reconfirm the JTBD or capability support.

## Expected Direction-Setting Output

The proxy round should produce:

1. The primary stewardship progress and lifecycle boundary.
2. An idea-recognition and minimum-evidence contract.
3. Senior judgment, ownership, and conflict-resolution rules.
4. Explicit stewardship responses, review forums, and re-entry memory.
5. Safe coordination rules and selected success measures.
6. Concrete episodes and a short current-banker validation set.

## CPIPE01 - Primary Progress

**Question:** What should disciplined pipeline stewardship enable a Senior Coverage MD and opportunity team to do more reliably?

**Response mode:** Select and rank up to three primary outcomes; classify the remainder as enablers or downstream benefits.

**Possible answers:**

- Know what is real and current without false precision.
- Recognize viable ideas earlier.
- Maintain purposeful client and opportunity movement.
- Direct franchise effort toward consequential pursuits.
- Make deliberate portfolio choices.
- Protect the outlook.
- Prevent silent opportunity leakage.
- Establish accountable stewardship.
- Reduce reconstruction and reporting overhead.
- Another outcome.

## CPIPE02 - Lifecycle Boundary

**Question:** Where should opportunity stewardship begin and end, and when should work remain in relationship management or move into detailed deal execution?

**Response mode:** Choose the closest lifecycle model and define entry, handoff, parking, and exit conditions.

**Possible answers:**

- One Idea-to-Closed continuum with maturity-specific evidence and decisions.
- Distinct but linked origination and active-deal jobs.
- Relationship management until explicit client interest exists.
- Product-specific boundaries for different transaction types.
- Adaptive boundary based on evidence, client intent, and execution commitment.
- Another model.

## CPIPE03 - Idea Recognition Threshold

**Question:** What is the minimum evidence for a plausible idea to enter shared stewardship without filling the portfolio with speculation?

**Response mode:** Select the minimum threshold and identify what may remain qualitative.

**Possible answers:**

- A plausible client need or strategic hypothesis.
- Relevant intelligence, interaction, or market evidence.
- Credible JPM role or differentiated contribution.
- Potential client or franchise value.
- A named accountable steward.
- A purposeful next learning or client move.
- An explicit uncertainty statement.
- A reason the idea matters now.
- Another threshold.

## CPIPE04 - Minimum Trusted Evidence And Plural Views

**Question:** What evidence is required at each maturity, and when may several legitimate views coexist?

**Response mode:** Select the core evidence and define how canonical convergence should increase with maturity.

**Possible answers:**

- Client need or strategic hypothesis.
- Evidence of client intent.
- Strategic and economic value, qualitative where necessary.
- Current lifecycle state and trajectory.
- JPM role and competitive position.
- Accountable owner and next client outcome.
- Blockers, dependencies, and decision window.
- Provenance, freshness, confidence, and sensitivity.
- Alternative interpretations with named owners.
- Another evidence element.

## CPIPE05 - Irreducible Senior Judgment

**Question:** Which pipeline judgments genuinely require the Senior Coverage MD rather than routine team administration?

**Response mode:** Select and rank the irreducible judgments.

**Possible answers:**

- Which opportunities merit continued pursuit.
- What client outcome should be pursued next.
- Whether the opportunity judgment has changed.
- Where franchise effort should move.
- When senior intervention can alter trajectory.
- When to reshape, monitor, park, reactivate, or stop.
- How institution-level relationship consequences affect pursuit.
- Whether conflicting product or regional views require escalation.
- Another judgment.

## CPIPE06 - Accountability Across GIB

**Question:** How should opportunity ownership and institution-level orchestration work across Coverage, products, regions, and KDMs?

**Response mode:** Choose the closest accountability model and define conflict resolution.

**Possible answers:**

- Coverage owns institution-level coherence; one opportunity owner accepts stewardship.
- One named opportunity owner leads regardless of organizational boundary.
- Best-positioned banker leads under a Coverage framework.
- Shared senior ownership with one coordinator.
- Ownership changes explicitly with lifecycle stage.
- Product teams own product pursuits while Coverage owns client posture and sequencing.
- Another model.

## CPIPE07 - Stewardship Responses And Movement

**Question:** Which explicit responses should follow a challenge-tested opportunity judgment?

**Response mode:** Select all legitimate states and define the minimum next-movement contract.

**Possible answers:**

- Advance toward a clearer client outcome.
- Reshape the hypothesis, product, team, or engagement path.
- Continue learning under an explicit condition.
- Monitor for a defined trigger.
- Park with re-entry conditions and a watch owner.
- Reactivate after new evidence.
- Exit deliberately with a minimal rationale.
- Escalate ownership or resource conflict.
- Route detailed commitments into execution or Actions.
- Another response.

## CPIPE08 - Review Forums And Challenge

**Question:** What should recurring and event-driven pipeline reviews produce, and which decisions belong in which forum?

**Response mode:** Select the legitimate purposes and identify authority, participants, and exit criteria.

**Possible answers:**

- Establish a shared current picture.
- Challenge maturity, trajectory, and assumptions.
- Decide pursuit, reshape, park, or exit.
- Resolve ownership and client-contact conflicts.
- Direct senior attention or franchise effort.
- Surface capacity or dependency constraints.
- Confirm next movement and review condition.
- Review only materially changed opportunities.
- Another purpose.

## CPIPE09 - Lost, Parked, And Reactivated Memory

**Question:** What should be retained when an opportunity is lost, declined, or parked so re-entry and learning are possible without permanent clutter?

**Response mode:** Select the minimum closure and reactivation record.

**Possible answers:**

- Outcome or park reason and supporting evidence.
- Last accepted opportunity judgment.
- Reactivation conditions or watch signals.
- Future-watch owner.
- Client and relationship consequences.
- Relevant objections or competitive outcome.
- Minimal lessons for future pursuits.
- Retention period and sensitivity classification.
- Another element.

## CPIPE10 - Restricted And Cross-GIB Coordination

**Question:** What minimum pipeline context may be shared when opportunity substance is restricted, and how should relevant evidence reach the accountable owner?

**Response mode:** Define the safe minimum, entitled detail, and escalation path.

**Possible answers:**

- That a restricted situation exists.
- Accountable owner and permitted routing path.
- High-level client or franchise consequence.
- A safe watch condition or do-not-contact instruction.
- Entitled substance only within the restricted group.
- Sanitized interpretation from the source owner.
- Legal, compliance, clean-team, or information-barrier review.
- No visibility where even existence is restricted.
- Another rule.

## CPIPE11 - Success And Failure

**Question:** What would prove pipeline stewardship is improving the business, and what must it avoid?

**Response mode:** Select one or two leading measures, one or two lagging outcomes, and the most important guardrails.

**Possible success measures:**

- Earlier recognition of viable ideas.
- Reliable accountable stewardship.
- More purposeful progression, parking, and exit.
- Faster recognition of changed trajectory.
- Improved stage-appropriate portfolio accuracy.
- Less management reconstruction and reporting effort.
- Fewer viable opportunities lost without review.

**Possible failure modes:**

- False precision or forced probability.
- Inflated idea volume without stewardship.
- Stale stages or optimistic reporting.
- Nominal ownership.
- Activity mistaken for client progress.
- Permanent clutter from weak or parked pursuits.
- Restricted-information leakage.

## CPIPE12 - Concrete Opportunity Episode

**Question:** Walk through one opportunity that was lost, nearly lost, parked, reactivated, or advanced later than it should have been. How did it first become visible, how was it stewarded, and where did the chain break or recover?

**Response mode:** Prefer a direct experience and label the cohort, product, region, and maturity. Do not infer a case where none is available.

**Probes:**

- What evidence first supported recognition?
- Which views differed, and when did they converge?
- Who accepted ownership and the next client outcome?
- Which review or event changed the trajectory?
- Were sensitivity, coordination, or resource constraints material?
- What should have become visible earlier?

## Proxy Synthesis And Banker Validation Gate

After proxy interviews, synthesize the lifecycle, recognition threshold, evidence and convergence contract, senior judgment boundary, accountability model, stewardship states, forum purposes, measures, disagreements, and episode evidence. Current bankers should receive no more than five focused validation questions and at least one real opportunity walkthrough; unresolved absence of a concrete episode must remain explicit.
