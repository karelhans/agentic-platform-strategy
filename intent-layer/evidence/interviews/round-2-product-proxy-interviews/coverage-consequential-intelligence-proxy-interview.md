# Coverage Consequential Intelligence - Proxy Interview

## Interview Record

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Interview ID** | `INT-COV-INT-001` |
| **Question range** | CINT01-CINT12 |
| **Status** | `Draft` |
| **Direction-setting audience** | Former senior Coverage bankers and intelligence, research, product, and information-flow SMEs |
| **Focused validation audience** | Current Senior Coverage MDs after proxy synthesis |
| **Purpose** | Validate the direction, process boundary, ownership, routing, and success tests for consequential intelligence without asking current bankers to repeat the original discovery. |
| **Existing foundation** | The Q91 JTBD is confirmed for Coverage: isolate consequential change, understand client-specific implications and evidential strength, then choose the response and intended outcome. The supporting process and contributor roles remain evidence-backed and revisable. |

## Interviewer Guidance

- Share all twelve questions in advance.
- Ask respondents to label each answer as direct experience, observed practice, or informed hypothesis.
- Seek the strongest choices rather than accepting every plausible answer.
- Treat this as broader consequential intelligence from any source. Use the separate Market Intelligence interview for market-specific domains and production.
- Preserve disagreement about source, ownership, and thresholds for focused banker validation.
- Proxy responses establish candidate direction only. They do not reconfirm the JTBD or prove capability support.

## Expected Direction-Setting Output

The proxy round should produce:

1. The primary progress and scope of consequential intelligence.
2. Materiality, context, evidence, and uncertainty tests.
3. A candidate preparatory-triage and senior-judgment boundary.
4. A disposition and destination-routing model.
5. Ownership, sensitivity, and feedback-loop requirements.
6. Leading measures, lagging outcomes, and failure guardrails.
7. A short current-banker validation set centered on disputed thresholds and a real episode.

## CINT01 - Primary Progress

**Question:** What should excellent intelligence triage enable a Senior Coverage MD to do more reliably than today?

**Response mode:** Select and rank up to three primary outcomes; classify the remainder as enablers or downstream benefits.

**Possible answers:**

- Isolate consequential change from noise.
- Understand which clients or franchise outcomes may be affected.
- Judge evidential strength without false certainty.
- Recognize a useful intervention window earlier.
- Choose an appropriate response and intended client outcome.
- Confidently decide not to act when evidence does not justify it.
- Avoid consequential surprises.
- Reduce senior time spent finding and validating information.
- Another outcome.

## CINT02 - Intelligence Scope

**Question:** Which information belongs in the consequential-intelligence job, and which should stay in specialist or destination-lens workflows?

**Response mode:** Rank the primary source classes; mark the remainder secondary, on-demand, or out of scope.

**Possible answers:**

- Market, macro, sector, and research developments.
- Client statements, behavior, and interaction evidence.
- Relationship changes and stakeholder movement.
- Opportunity, transaction, and competitive evidence.
- Product, regional, and cross-JPM activity.
- Regulatory, policy, legal, and geopolitical developments.
- Internal commitments, blockers, and execution changes.
- External news, peer actions, and competitor intelligence.
- Another source class.

## CINT03 - Materiality And Timing

**Question:** What makes new information consequential enough to enter senior Coverage triage?

**Response mode:** Select and rank the strongest tests; explain whether several must combine.

**Possible answers:**

- Material client or franchise consequence.
- A short or closing decision window.
- Relationship sensitivity or trust implications.
- A meaningful change to an opportunity judgment.
- Strategic ambiguity requiring senior context.
- Cross-client, cross-product, or cross-region consequence.
- Contradiction of the team's accepted view.
- Costly or difficult-to-reverse delay.
- Another materiality test.

## CINT04 - Noise And Duplication

**Question:** What should happen before information is treated as a distinct intelligence item rather than repetition, commentary, or background context?

**Response mode:** Select the minimum preprocessing required before contextual assessment.

**Possible answers:**

- Deduplicate equivalent facts or reports.
- Separate new evidence from repeated commentary.
- Connect the item to existing knowledge or an active watch condition.
- Identify what materially changed from the prior understood state.
- Exclude information with no plausible client or franchise relevance.
- Preserve multiple sources where corroboration or disagreement matters.
- Let the relevant owner decide whether apparent duplication is meaningful.
- Another rule.

## CINT05 - Client And Franchise Translation

**Question:** What context must be added before intelligence is useful for senior Coverage judgment?

**Response mode:** Select the minimum required context and identify what may remain on demand.

**Possible answers:**

- Affected client, stakeholder, opportunity, or franchise scope.
- Prior client and relationship context.
- Existing hypothesis or accepted judgment that may change.
- Potential consequence and remaining response window.
- Client usability: what could be learned, discussed, protected, or advanced.
- Relevant ownership, commitments, or dependencies.
- JPM relevance and credible right to respond.
- Known restrictions or sensitivity boundaries.
- Another context element.

## CINT06 - Evidence And Uncertainty

**Question:** What evidence contract should govern potentially important but uncertain intelligence?

**Response mode:** Select the essential trust elements and state how standards change with consequence and time.

**Possible answers:**

- Named source and visible provenance.
- Timestamp and freshness appropriate to the decision window.
- Clear distinction between fact, inference, and opinion.
- Corroboration or explanation of why one source is sufficient.
- Explicit confidence and missing facts.
- Alternative interpretations or dissenting evidence.
- Sensitivity and permitted-use classification.
- Proportionately greater scrutiny for higher consequence.
- Use as a question or hypothesis where action would be premature.
- Another requirement.

## CINT07 - Preparatory Triage Ownership

**Question:** What should VPs, associates, product partners, research, or intelligence contributors complete before direct MD attention is requested?

**Response mode:** Select the minimum preparatory work and identify one accountable preparatory owner or stage-dependent model.

**Possible answers:**

- Reduce noise and duplication.
- Identify affected clients and existing contexts.
- Explain the potential consequence and timing.
- Expose source, evidence, confidence, and uncertainty.
- Distinguish what is known, inferred, and restricted.
- Prepare response options or a specific senior question.
- Identify the current owner and destination if already clear.
- Escalate immediately where delay is itself consequential.
- Another preparatory responsibility.

## CINT08 - Irreducible MD Judgment

**Question:** Once preparatory triage is complete, which judgments must remain with the Senior Coverage MD?

**Response mode:** Select the irreducible judgments, not engagement mechanics that can be delegated.

**Possible answers:**

- Whether the information materially matters in context.
- Whether it is credible enough for the contemplated use.
- Whether a response is warranted now.
- What client or franchise outcome should be pursued.
- Whether an existing relationship or opportunity judgment has changed.
- Whether personal MD involvement is required.
- Whether deliberate monitoring or non-action is appropriate.
- Another senior judgment.

## CINT09 - Disposition And Routing

**Question:** What explicit dispositions should complete intelligence triage, and where should accepted intelligence go next?

**Response mode:** Select all legitimate dispositions, then define their destination or reassessment condition.

**Possible answers:**

- Dismiss as noise with rationale.
- Retain as context.
- Monitor for a defined condition.
- Validate or investigate further.
- Route to relationship management.
- Route to opportunity pipeline.
- Route to meeting preparation.
- Route to an action, commitment, or accountable owner.
- Create a temporary cross-lens situation.
- Act directly or prepare engagement.
- Another disposition or destination.

## CINT10 - Sensitivity And Safe Sharing

**Question:** How should intelligence be coordinated when relevant evidence cannot be shared fully across teams or regions?

**Response mode:** Define the safe minimum, accountable routing, and escalation model.

**Possible answers:**

- Share that a restricted situation exists and identify its owner.
- Share a permitted consequence or watch condition without source substance.
- Route evidence only to an entitled destination owner.
- Ask the source owner to provide a sanitized interpretation.
- Use legal, compliance, clean-team, or information-barrier review.
- Suppress cross-linking where even existence is restricted.
- Preserve an audit trail of access and disposition.
- Another safe-sharing rule.

## CINT11 - Success And Failure

**Question:** What would prove consequential-intelligence triage is improving senior Coverage work, and what must it avoid?

**Response mode:** Select one or two leading measures, one or two lagging outcomes, and the most important guardrails.

**Possible success measures:**

- Earlier identification of relevant change.
- Faster evidence-to-disposition time.
- Better client conversations or decisions.
- Fewer consequential surprises.
- Less senior time spent on noise and manual verification.
- More appropriate deliberate non-action.
- Faster movement in the destination lens.

**Possible failure modes:**

- Alert volume without consequence.
- Missing client context.
- False confidence or hidden uncertainty.
- Duplicated investigation.
- Late escalation.
- Restricted-information leakage.
- Intelligence recorded without an owner, disposition, or destination.

## CINT12 - Representative Episode

**Question:** Describe one recent intelligence item that materially changed, or should have changed, a client or franchise decision. Where did it originate, how was it interpreted, who judged it, and what happened next?

**Response mode:** Use a direct experience where possible; otherwise label the example as observed practice or hypothesis.

**Probes:**

- What was the first clue and when did it become consequential?
- Which context was missing or had to be reconstructed?
- What evidence and uncertainty were visible?
- Who owned preparation, senior judgment, and downstream action?
- Was the final disposition act, route, monitor, or dismiss?
- What would have improved the outcome or reduced wasted attention?

## Proxy Synthesis And Banker Validation Gate

After proxy interviews, synthesize the proposed triage contract, preparatory-owner model, senior judgment boundary, disposition set, routing rules, measures, disagreements, and evidence gaps. Current bankers should receive no more than five focused validation questions, including at least one concrete episode and any disputed materiality or trust threshold.
