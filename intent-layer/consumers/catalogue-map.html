The catalogue today, intent layer and product layer

Two pictures of the same thing. The first is the intent layer as it stands on 2026-10-07: five job families, their outcomes, jobs, business use cases, scenarios and actors, with every candidate and superseded record marked. The second is the product layer as IBIQ uses it today: five product use cases, each placed under the family it cites, plus the personas, capabilities and the alignment statement still to be written.

1. The intent layer

Read each column from the top. The outcome says what should change. The family is the standing area of work. The jobs explain why bankers do it. The use cases are the processes, each with a value delivered, and the scenarios are the conditions each process meets. Solid records are admitted. Dashed amber records are candidates from the model revision of 2026-10-07: cite them only as gaps. Grey records are superseded: do not cite them.

2. The product layer

The product layer is owned by the product and cites the intent layer by ID. A product use case names the business use case and steps it changes, one scenario it covers and one it leaves alone, and the actor behind its persona. A capability is justified only by the product use cases it enables. The alignment statement is the written answer to the nine litmus tests.
